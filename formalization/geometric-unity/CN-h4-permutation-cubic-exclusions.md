# CN. Two permutation-character exclusions for H4

On the real pure H4 branch of the flat positive observer, the
grade-five equations have only the zero value in each of the
following subspaces of the curvature kernel. The result also holds
after complexification.

Embed \(S_5\) in \(SO(5)\) by \(P\mapsto\det(P)P\) for permutation
matrices \(P\). This acts on \(V=\operatorname{Sym}^2_0\mathbb R^5\)
by ordinary permutation conjugation. A superscript \(+\) denotes
the fixed space and \(-\) the sign space. A multiplicity in the
table includes all copies of that type in the
[BX](BX-h4-curvature-and-closed-fields.md) curvature kernel.

| Character | Types and copy counts | Dimension |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Fixed | \((4,0),\,2(3,3),\,3(3,1),\,(2,0),\,4(4,2),\,2(5,1),\,4(5,2)\) | \(3+2+3+1+12+6+4=31\) |
| Sign | \(6(3,2),\,4(3,0),\,2(1,0),\,6(4,1)\) | \(6+8+2+12=28\) |

The grade-one baseline requires positive norm. Thus no full field
can take a value in either support or in their compact rotation
unions. Mixtures with other values can have mixed quadratic terms.

## CN1. Exact domains

The [verifier](../../computation/geometric-unity/CN_h4_s5_cubic.py)
uses rational physical-frame representatives. It checks both hook
traces, the complete \(R^{T^*}\wedge\star C\) equation, the compact
Casimir eigenvalue, and the action of each of the four adjacent
transpositions on every tensor. It obtains the latter actions
directly from permutation conjugation of the physical frame.
Nonzero minors prove independence on each declared support.

The dimension count uses the \(B_2\) Kostant weight formula with
positive roots \((1,-1),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)\).
On each of the seven conjugacy classes, the embedding into
\(SO(5)\) has two rotation eigenvalue pairs and one eigenvalue
\(1\). The verifier evaluates the weight character at those pairs
by reduction modulo the pertinent cyclotomic polynomial.
Averaging over the classes gives the stated fixed and sign
dimensions. It also sums over any other curvature type with the
same Casimir eigenvalue. In particular \((3,3)\) and \((4,1)\)
share the eigenvalue \(30\), and \((4,1)\) contributes zero to the
trivial restriction. This checks that the independent bases span
the complete stated domains. The full fixed and sign spaces have
dimensions 68 and 110.

## CN2. Cubic multiples of the actual equations

Write \(C=\sum_i x_i C_i\) for one rational basis. Each selected
receiver is an actual external index and an increasing five-blade.
Diagonal values and ordinary polarization give the quadratic
coefficient matrix, with columns \(x_i x_j\) for \(i\le j\).
The matrices have full row rank modulo \(65{,}521\). Their
nonzero nullities show that this degree-two linear test alone
does not prove the assertion.

| Domain | Quadratic shape | Quadratic nullity | Cubic shape | Cubic rank |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Trivial 31 | \(195\times496\) | 301 | \(6045\times5456\) | 5456 |
| Sign 28 | \(187\times406\) | 219 | \(5236\times4060\) | 4060 |

The cubic rows are \(x_k\) times each selected quadratic. The
verifier finds a full-column minor with nonzero determinant in
the same finite field. Every coefficient denominator is
invertible at the prime. The same minors are therefore nonzero
over \(\mathbb Q\), and the cubic rows span all degree-three
monomials there. In particular each \(x_i^3\) belongs to the
ideal generated by the grade-five equations. Their only common
complex zero on either support is the origin.

The coefficient calculation uses the independent 128-dimensional
matrix realization of [CK](CK-h4-invariant-grade-five.md).
Metric square roots in the finite field convert to an orthonormal
frame and are removed again upon extraction in the rational
coframe. Every evaluation checks the Clifford hook, the scalar
trace and a matrix product in integer arithmetic. On each support,
12 receiver values are also reconstructed by BC's original
rational nested Clifford expression. The modular Schur steps use
exact integer-valued binary64 products. Their dot lengths are
bounded by 64 and the entries are reduced after each block
product, so \(64(65521-1)^2+65521<2^{53}\). Each pivot block
inverse is verified with integer products.

## Reproduction

    python -X utf8 computation/geometric-unity/CN_h4_s5_cubic.py --wide

The default selected check verifies the 16-dimensional sign and
21-dimensional fixed subsupports obtained by omitting the added
types \((4,1)\) and \((5,1),(5,2)\), respectively. The cubic ranks
there are 816 and 1771. Use \(\texttt{--wide}\) for the two
maximal supports reported here and \(\texttt{--receipt}\) to save
the modular minor selections.
