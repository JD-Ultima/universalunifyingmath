# BP. Four Cartan-square obstructions in the H6 kernel

In the curvature kernel of [BO](BO-h6-full-curvature-kernel.md), the
sum of types $(3,2),(3,0),(2,1),(2,0)$ has dimension 184. Its complexification
has no nonzero zero of the homogeneous grade-nine quadratic. On the
real branch of [BN](BN-h6-invariant-grade-nine.md), the positive grade-one
baseline also excludes the zero value, so the whole 184-dimensional
submodule is excluded from the full equation.

The [verifier](../../computation/geometric-unity/BP_h6_cartan_grade9.py)
transports four of BO's extremal vectors to the physical coframe and
computes one exact nonzero output coordinate for each. The
[review](../../reports/reviews/REVIEW80-GU-BP-2026-11-14.md) records
the independent controls.

## BP1. A nonzero Cartan-square projection

Let $V_\lambda$ be a complex irreducible module of dominant weight
$\lambda$. Its Cartan summand in $\operatorname{Sym}^2 V_\lambda$ is
the unique copy of $V_{2\lambda}$, generated by the square of a
highest vector. The projection of $v\otimes v$ to this summand
is nonzero for every $v\ne0$. Indeed, let $f$ be a highest covector
in the dual representation. The square $f\otimes f$ lies in the
dual Cartan summand. If the projection of $v\otimes v$ vanished,
every translate of $f\otimes f$ would annihilate it. Then
$(gf)(v)^2=0$ for all group elements $g$. Since the orbit of $f$
spans the irreducible dual, this forces $v=0$.

In particular, an equivariant quadratic $q$ on $V_\lambda$ that
takes a nonzero value on a highest vector has nonzero projection to
its output isotypic component of type $2\lambda$ for every $v\ne0$.
Equivariance places the nonzero highest-vector image in a copy of
that type, and the restriction of the polarized map to the Cartan
summand is injective.

## BP2. Four grade-nine calibrations

Complexify the real coefficient map $q_9=Q_9(iB\Gamma)/i$ from BN.
The equivariant map evaluated on each exact BO highest vector has the
following physical coframe coefficient. A mask encodes an
increasing-index nine-form in the coframe of BC.

| Input weight | Doubled weight | External index | Mask | Coefficient |
|:---|:---|---:|---:|:---|
| $(3,2)$ | $(6,4)$ | 10 | 14709 | $-399015$ |
| $(3,0)$ | $(6,0)$ | 4 | 10367 | $-12623i/2$ |
| $(2,1)$ | $(4,2)$ | 13 | 10997 | $-7449791/30$ |
| $(2,0)$ | $(4,0)$ | 9 | 8927 | $-431i/5$ |

The coframe conversion uses the inverse of the vector basis matrix
in every slot. For the complex value $B=R+iI$, compute the three real
quadratics on $R,I,R+I$. Then
$$
q_9(R+iI)=q_9(R)-q_9(I)+i\big(q_9(R+I)-q_9(R)-q_9(I)\big).
$$
The zero tests on both real hooks and the full curvature map are also
included. The computation retains BN's physical volume normalization.

## BP3. Descending weight elimination

With the simple roots $(1,-1),(0,1)$, any highest weight $\nu$
in $V_\lambda\otimes V_\mu$ satisfies
$$
\nu_1\le \lambda_1+\mu_1,\qquad
\nu_1+\nu_2\le\lambda_1+\lambda_2+\mu_1+\mu_2.
$$
Among the four types listed, the target $(6,4)$ can receive contributions
only from the square of $(3,2)$, since other pairs have total weight
at most eight. If $q_9(B)=0$, BP1 and the first calibration force
that input component to vanish. Among the three remaining types
only the square of $(3,0)$ can reach first coordinate six. Its
$(6,0)$ target forces that input to vanish. In the remaining two types,
only the square of $(2,1)$ can reach total weight six. Its $(4,2)$
target forces the third component to vanish. The $(4,0)$ target
forces the last component to vanish.

The resulting complex obstruction on these four types implies the
claimed real field exclusion under BN's hypotheses. Other irreducible
types can contribute to the same output components, so the argument
does not extend to mixtures with arbitrary elements of the full
6,834-dimensional kernel. External peer review remains outstanding.
