## 2. Cross-framework findings

1. **Sign and convention choices are load-bearing in all three.**
   - Chain Theory selects its unique link only under one far-end conjugation convention (F-CT-3); that convention follows from requiring the identification algebra to contain the matchings, but a further clause at equal dimensions is still an input (`conjugation.md`).
   - GU's (7,7) signature needs $\lambda>\tfrac14$ and, for a Lorentzian base, a fixed relative block sign (a base of signature (2,2) gives (7,7) for either sign); its gauge-equivariance lemmas hold only after one global sign choice that changes at least two printed formulas (C3′).
   - Howard's no-go depends on reading $2\otimes2=4$ from one sentence.

   In each case the repo states the result separately for each choice, so the dependence is explicit rather than hidden.
2. **Two routes to the same group theory.** Chain Theory gets $S(U(2)\times U(3))\subset SU(5)$ from the $2\otimes3$ link. GU uses the maximal compact symmetry of $\mathrm{Spin}(6,4)$, covered by the Pati–Salam factors $\mathrm{Spin}(6)\times\mathrm{Spin}(4)$, and a 16 of Spin(10) type from one Majorana–Weyl spinor. These are the two classical maximal-subgroup routes into Spin(10) (Georgi–Glashow vs Pati–Salam; Baez–Huerta 2010). These connections use the established group embeddings cited here; they establish representation relationships within the specified reconstructions.
   - **Cross-constraint.** Under Chain Theory's convention A the link carries $(3,2)_{1/6}$, the off-diagonal block of $\Lambda^2\mathbf5=\mathbf{10}$, a matter multiplet that sits in GU's 16. Under A* it carries $(\bar3,2)_{5/6}$, the off-diagonal block of $\mathrm{End}\,\mathbf5=\mathbf{24}+\mathbf1$, which sits in the Spin(10) adjoint 45 (gauge sector). Requiring CT's identification algebra to contain the matchings selects A (`conjugation.md`). So within these embeddings the photon link is "matter" unless that requirement is dropped, which is what A* needs.
3. **Numerical comparisons depend on the specified identification and uncertainty.**
   - The α Letter's value is 0.6σ from CODATA 2022. A declared 46,080-rule space contains the paper's twelve weight-law alternatives, and the Letter's value is the closest in that family. Under an explicitly artificial uniform-random-target model on (120,155), the expected match count in a ±1σ window is approximately $4.4\times10^{-6}$ (F-CT-4). This is a model-dependent reference calculation, not a calibrated significance for the construction. A second, mechanically generated family (332 308 single and paired mutants of the author's Lean definitions) also has the Letter's value as its only value within relative $10^{-4}$; its reference count in the same model is $1.3\times10^{-6}$ (F-CT-4 E). The available source does not document its rule-selection history.
   - The compendium's numerical comparisons depend on the adopted identification: 11 of 16 adopted numerical readings differ by more than a factor of ten, while other readings give closer values for some quantities. Unselected printed readings include $m_p/m_e=(2871/67)^2$ ($+1.8\times10^{-5}$), $m_\mu/m_e$ ($-0.09\%$), and on-shell $\sin^2\theta_W=(17/36)^2$ ($-0.8\sigma$). The local-density reference counts depend on the illustrative models in the scorecard and mass-cell analyses and do not establish significance. The derivation audit records where candidate numbers and face choices enter; their relationship to a physical mechanism remains a separate question.
   - The Howard analysis records exact arithmetic identities, incompatible numerical statements under specified readings, and questions that require clearer definitions or primary sources.
4. **Formal verification checks the definitions, not the physics.** The Lean coverage in both Chain Theory sources certifies finite arithmetic of definitions, and in the compendium often only label counts. The compendium tree (493 modules, core only) does not build under Lean 4.35.0 (it pins 4.32.1, not available here): `decide` fails at two proofs, and at three more once those are patched, a toolchain incompatibility; with these five proofs changed to `rfl` it builds with no `sorry`, but for 10 of its 16 "restated/kernel" theorem ties the cited Lean states the content of a different box or of none (compendium round 6). Over all 64 Lean-tied theorem rows, 1 is matched clause by clause, 7 partly and 56 not at all, counting a clause when some part of its finite content is stated; counting entered conclusions as round 6 did gives 1 / 8 / 55, and requiring all of a clause's finite content gives 0 / 7 / 57, the single match (9.4) resting on a relation over an entered step table (corrected after review 2026-10-30, R4-CT-8; see `reports/CORRECTIONS.md`); 560 of the 618 table-check lemmas only confirm that an entered key/value string occurs (round 7). Our own Lean files (Chain Theory convention check and class-function theorem; GU tilt-sign and Stueckelberg lemmas; Howard arithmetic) are stated with that limitation in mind.

## 4. Next steps, in priority order

1. **GU singular branches.** R10 closes additions to fixed constant Euclidean solved backgrounds, including R9's example. Determine the odd equation for nonconstant $(t,u)$ moving along the non-vector singular curve, with $T_e=\Sigma(dt,du)^\top+k$. Fields crossing $\det M=0$, indefinite real forms, and odd fields outside the invariant sector remain separate open problems. R10's positivity argument does not decide them.
2. **GU remaining Z1 case.** In S15's twisted cone at $\lambda=1/5$, (14,0), away from the parallel set, determine whether any rank-at-most-one Ricci base jet can satisfy the relaxed integrability system on a fibre-open set. One fully specified quadratic metric jet is now excluded by an exact augmented-rank check and the nonzero-minor argument of S4; extend that obstruction or find a consistent jet. This example does not classify the rank-one sector. Preserve the source's unresolved choices of Z1/Z2, the off-section field rule, and fibre weight/system; the section variation beyond the invariant sector remains open.
3. **Chain Theory defining inputs.** CT-G02 still asks for an independent principle fixing the weight attribution. CT-G03/G04 reduce to matching closure and the stated proper-algebra condition. CT-G09 requires the stated order/identification readings and, covariantly, the qubit identification. The recent reviews reproduce their conditional mathematics; they do not establish an axiom-level reason for those inputs.
4. **Chain Theory search scope and compendium dependencies.** Mechanical mutation checks remain bounded by the declared mutation depth; reference counts do not establish statistical significance. Resolve per-box premises for shared derivations and the remaining any-of citations before strengthening dependency conclusions. Physical comparisons require explicit identification maps.
5. **Howard primary definitions.** Obtain the primary book and full relevant patent texts, then rerun the consistency analysis on the operations and products actually defined there. Missing sources remain a documented limitation.
