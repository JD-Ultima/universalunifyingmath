## November 5 comparison: where to continue

Live GitHub revision checks left the SproutSeeds audit/math-lab, locality port, author source, steelman and Manuel pins above unchanged. Only the inspected disruptionjoe head advanced. No upstream suite or build was repeated. Search queries covered the named theories, actual H2 affine fields, H6 curvature hooks, SO(5) Casimir modules and covariantly closed double forms. The disruptionjoe comparison examined selected current statements and its research index, not every file at the new head; negative keyword matches carry little weight.

No corresponding positive all-parameter affine H2 exclusion, H6 fixed-value rank/obstruction or degree-two-module obstruction was identified in those sources. That bounded finding supports continuing [BG](../formalization/geometric-unity/BG-h6-fixed-values.md)/[BH](../formalization/geometric-unity/BH-h6-degree2.md); it does not establish literature priority. Private, unindexed and uninspected work remains outside this check. Credit the component overlap above, reuse the already verified read cap, and compare definitions before repeating another audit.

The underlying geometry and methods are established mathematics. The [Gaussian symmetric-space sources](../sources/geometric-unity.md#4-classical-geometry-used-in-the-global-models) already credit the metric identification. Labbi's [double-form Laplacian paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.12828v1), especially its covariant-derivative identities and Theorems A–C, provides related curvature methods. Its stated compactness, form-type and positive-curvature hypotheses do not directly supply our noncompact H6 setting with negative Ricci curvature. No general vanishing theorem was imported from it. Spherical harmonics, Casimir eigenvalues and compact-group averaging are standard tools, not originality claims.
