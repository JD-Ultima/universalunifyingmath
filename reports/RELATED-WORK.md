# Related work and reuse

Survey dated November 2, 2026, with formal verification updated November 3. We inspected the pinned public sources below, including theorem statements, implementations and recorded checks. This is a bounded comparison, not an exhaustive literature search or a novelty claim. Passing a formal or computational check establishes its stated mathematics; it does not confirm the proposed physics.

Most entries are source inspections. We separately reproduced two Python calculations after source review: SproutSeeds' pinned record counter returned **169 products, 172 records and sectors 79/48/42/3**, agreeing with our independently implemented matrix counter; and **10 direct exact Python/SymPy controls** checked the steelman repository's B01 signature routines—a symbolic spectrum, six rational inertias, two chimeric signatures and a base-mirror identity. No complete upstream Python suite was run. The November 3 [read-cap target rebuild](chain-theory-readcap-reuse.md) and adapter check add fresh formal verification; other upstream build records remain attributed records.

## Verified reuse and remaining interpretation

The [fresh read-cap verification](chain-theory-readcap-reuse.md) compiled the pinned Woronowicz closure and five small adapters with standard axiom reports. Math-lab's operator predicate matches the Letter, and its cap directly fits the author's abstract [`ReadCap`](https://github.com/physical-superintelligence/chain-theory/blob/a28db8998bce5bd5ef11195c05f13c86cd9dac5c/papers/alpha/lean/ChainAlpha.lean#L510). A separate [selection theorem](https://github.com/SproutSeeds/math-lab/blob/48d14da643ec5f6e40ea0e2ed9f2a5eb9c8d5fda/MathLab/ChainTheory/LocalUnitary.lean#L259) reaches the same dimension conclusion; it was inspected but was not part of our rebuild. The rebuild uses official pinned Mathlib cache artifacts and does not repeat the entire MathLab suite.

Keep the author's lowercase `readable` distinct: it admits `(3,3)`, which operator readability excludes. Operational certainty, implementation of reads, physical identification and the weight rules remain unresolved modelling inputs. Licenses and proof provenance remain explicit.

[Kleis at `20ed142`](https://github.com/eatikrh/kleis/tree/20ed1427f3c8ec0bd241444600d4a7d2820282bd) is also useful for a claim/premise ledger, but its inspected obstruction conclusions depend on explicitly assumed bridges. Its shadow-model assignments do not establish independence from the full GU construction; its non-commercial license also requires separate reuse consideration.

Our current basis remains in the [synthesis](SYNTHESIS.md). [AW](../formalization/geometric-unity/AW-lambda5-conic-classification.md) combines the first-conic obstruction with a new normal-moment/conservation argument to close both single Lambda5 actual-domain cases within this reconstruction. The inspected external implementations did not supply that completed result; this is a bounded comparison, not a literature-first claim. Standard geometric and Clifford methods and verified prior results remain credited with their hypotheses.
