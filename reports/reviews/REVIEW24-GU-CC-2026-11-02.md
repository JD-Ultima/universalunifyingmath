# Internal review of the diagonal Weyl plane

November 2, 2026. Review of `formalization/geometric-unity/CC-diagonal-weyl.md`, the fresh driver, rank helper, compact JSON certificates and recorded output. CC1–CC6 are valid. This is an internal mathematical review, not external peer review or a Lean formalization.

## Intrinsic scope

At the specified point, the hypotheses are

$$\mathrm{Ric}=\rho\,p^\flat\otimes p^\flat,\qquad
|p|=1,\quad\rho\ne0,\qquad
W(p,u,v,w)=0\quad(u,v,w\perp p).$$

I checked the claimed normal form. The matrix $E_{ij}=W_{0i0j}$ is symmetric and tracefree. The spatial three-dimensional curvature tensor has Ricci tensor $-E$ and scalar zero. In three dimensions the Ricci contraction determines the algebraic curvature tensor, giving $(-E)\owedge\delta$. Diagonalizing $E$ by an oriented perpendicular frame produces $(\alpha,\beta,-\alpha-\beta)$ and exactly the complementary sectional pattern in CC2. The assumed mixed components supply the remaining zeros. Conversely that pattern has vanishing mixed components. The condition is unchanged when the unit representative of the Ricci eigenline is reversed.

Normal coordinates then allow the actual cubic and quartic Taylor coefficients, with a smooth zero-four-jet remainder. Thus this is a curvature condition at one point, with arbitrary higher jets. It is not a classification of arbitrary Weyl curvature or Ricci-flat nonflat points.

## Degree bounds and the finite compatibility cover

The total-degree bounds are established before interpolation. The quadratic base coefficients are affine in two curvature parameters; the connection's linear and cubic terms have total degrees at most one and two. The observer inverse at the origin is parameter independent. Its first metric derivatives have degree at most one, and its second and third at most two. Consequently the relaxed coefficients have total degree at most two and the RHS at most three.

The six and ten triangular evaluation nodes are unisolvent, with absolute determinants 4 and 576. They therefore determine the respective polynomial entries exactly. The additional four nodes also check the selected coefficient reconstruction. No determinant degree or parameter range is inferred from finite sampling.

At each of the three stated numerical fibres, the sparse selected rows have only $\xi_{13}$ and a common metric combination of $n$ as unknowns. The exact compatibility ideals are the square of the point ideal at $(a,b)=(\sigma/6,\sigma/6)$. Adding the signed-rotation configuration gives the unit ideal for each sign. These are exact identities over $\mathbb Q[a,b]$, rather than numerical common-root tests.

The signed perpendicular swap has determinant one, preserves $R_0$, and sends

$$\mathcal W_A\mapsto \mathcal W_A-\mathcal W_B,\qquad \mathcal W_B\mapsto-\mathcal W_B.$$

It therefore implements $(a,b)\mapsto(a,-a-b)$. Its fourth configuration in the original frame is $\operatorname{diag}(2,3,5,4)$ with rows $(140,309,605)$, not an assumption about sparsity at arbitrary diagonal matrices. The rotated first numerator is nonzero at the sole original common zero for both Ricci signs.

## Uniform rank at the same configurations

The character decomposition is legitimate for the auxiliary quadratic background. All four independent coordinate reflections preserve its diagonal curvature tensor and a diagonal fibre matrix. Naturality of the observer metric and relaxed tensor system permits the full sign group. Restricting to determinant-one signs would identify complementary off-diagonal characters and would not justify six separate three-column blocks.

The unknowns split as a 12-column trivial block, six off-diagonal three-column blocks and four horizontal three-column blocks. The six-node support checks verify all off-block zeros within the proved degree bound. Arbitrary higher jets are not assumed to have these reflection symmetries.

I checked the verifier's rank logic and its use of the rational data:

- The trivial block has a constant rational left inverse, checked against all six fresh node matrices. The resulting polynomial identity proves rank 12 for every parameter.
- Each horizontal block has a reduced two-column constant left inverse. The corresponding nonzero universal kernel vector bounds its full rank by two, so its rank is exactly two.
- Each off-diagonal block has two constant output combinations of rank two and a fixed one-dimensional kernel. Two residual rows on this kernel are $c_u(3x+\sigma)^2$ and $c_v(6x-\sigma)^2$, with nonzero rational scales. The explicit Bézout identity in CC9, including those scales, proves that both residuals cannot vanish. Thus each block has rank three everywhere.

These are polynomial left-inverse and unit-ideal certificates, not assertions about sampled ranks. The disjoint blocks give rank $12+6\cdot3+4\cdot2=38$. The four independent columns $K$ therefore span the entire kernel at each of the three compatibility fibres, for both signs and every real parameter pair.

The three four-row completions restrict to invertible diagonal prefactors times the squares of

$$\sigma+3a,\qquad\sigma+3b,\qquad\sigma-3a-3b.$$

Their sum is $3\sigma\ne0$, so at least one completion eliminates all four kernel directions. The full coefficient rank is therefore 42 at every one of those same fibres. Naturality supplies the fourth configuration's rank. This closes the same-witness gap without a density argument or a general-diagonal sparsity claim.

## Higher jets and the local conclusion

The accepted BB/Z support proof applies to any fixed quadratic curvature. Cubic variations leave the 722-row subsystem unchanged; parity fixes the selected RHS rows; the diagonal-vertical quartic gradient cancellation fixes their quartic contribution. Higher remainders do not enter the required observer jets.

In each completion row, a cubic change affects only $\xi_O$. It vanishes on $K$, so the invertible completion restriction survives. Thus rank and incompatibility remain at the same finite configuration for arbitrary higher jets. The baseline character argument is not applied to the entire actual coefficient matrix after those jets vary.

For a fixed actual metric, rank 42 and inconsistency give an augmented 43-minor. Its rational fibre dependence excludes every fibre-open consistent patch. Continuing that particular minor at its positive coordinate fibre witness gives a base neighbourhood; nonzero Ricci also allows nonzero base curvature to persist there. The reviewed U5 constant-piece/dense-variable-patch argument then supplies the full-field consequence. The intrinsic curvature condition need only hold at the original point.

The latter consequence retains S.0: compact $(14,0)$ and $\mathfrak u(128)$, Z1 at $\lambda=1/5$, positive $c_H,c_V$, invariant odd field, slaved vector even field, the fixed reconstructed Shiab member, nonzero Einstein coefficients $(x,y)\ne(0,0)$, $\kappa\ne0$ and $\det M\ne0$. Arbitrary even fields and non-vector singular branches are outside it. No parameter-uniform neighbourhood is asserted.
