# Review81. Invariant moments on the full H6 kernel

**Date** 2026-11-14. **Verdict** Accept BQ1–BQ3 under their stated hypotheses.
[Proof](../../formalization/geometric-unity/BQ-h6-invariant-moments.md)
and [verifier](../../computation/geometric-unity/BQ_h6_invariant_forms.py).

The metric and the two quadratic pairings are invariant. Compact
conjugation of BO's rational split-frame highest vectors coincides
with the same diagonal reflection of two five-dimensional axes on
every copy. Hence their real parts carry a common irreducible vector
across a multiplicity space. Checking off-diagonal matrix entries
on sums of those real parts fixes the multiplicity phases. Schur's
lemma and the positive Gram metric then give the covariance formulas.

The grade-five receiver was reconstructed by ordered matrix powers
and checked for support, norm and vanishing under all ten generators.
The first calibration and separate six-product Clifford
loop compared all 48,006 coefficients exactly, including zero
coordinates, across 21 individual or polarized seed evaluations.
The two calculation routes share the physical frame, transported
seed tensors and declared Clifford formula. The public verifier
reconstructs the real seeds from BO's rational data and compares
selected coefficients with the independent nested engine. The
default verifier recalibrates three blocks, including a double
multiplicity, and checks every algebraic certificate. Its
`--full` mode checks every calibrated matrix.

Each support exclusion uses explicit strictly positive minors.
The upper energy bound uses a nonnegative matrix on all 15 blocks
and a positive norm profile attaining the bound within the two
scalar equations. This establishes the claimed bound without assuming
the full residual vanishes for that profile. External peer review
remains outstanding.
