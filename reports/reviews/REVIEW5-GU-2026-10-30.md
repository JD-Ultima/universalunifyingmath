## 1. Items

**R7-1 (error; invalid step, conclusion unaffected). J5d, Theorem L1 / Corollary L2: the trace $C_2$ is not determined by the leaf condition as written.**
- *Location:*
  - J5d note, notation, l. 26: "Both are linear in $F$, so $F\equiv0$ gives $C_1=C_2=0$";
  - Theorem L1, l. 61 ("In particular, $F\equiv0$ forces $\Lambda=0$") and l. 63 ("$\Lambda^2$ is an explicit $\mathbb Q$-linear combination …", "works because … $6:16$ and $\tfrac34:16$");
  - Corollary L2, proof step 3, l. 98: "By Theorem L1, $0=8C_2-C_1=112\Lambda_g^2$".
  - Repeated in:
    - `J-slaved-sector.md` J5(d), l. 199 ("Two traces of $F$ satisfy $8C_2-C_1=28|\mathrm{Ric}|^2$ … so $\Lambda_g=0$");
    - phase-1, l. 213 ("two traces of the order-$\varepsilon^2$ leaf condition give $8C_2-C_1=112\Lambda_g^2$");
    - J5d yaml `proof_status` (l. 25–28);
    - the label of `J5d_leaf.py` §L4 (l. 491);
    - the commit message of `38cc640`.
- *What is wrong:*
  1. L0(c) gives exactly this: $Q_{X,Y}(h):=F(X,Y;h,h)=0$ for all $X,Y,h$. Polarising in $h$ yields only the symmetric part:
     $$\tfrac12\big[F(X,Y;h,h')+F(X,Y;h',h)\big]=\tfrac12\big[F(X,Y;h,h')+F(Y,X;h,h')\big]=0 .$$
  2. As defined in the note, the bilinear form $F(X,Y;h,h')=\sum_ig^{ii}G_0(R(X,e_i)\cdot h,R(Y,e_i)\cdot h')$ is **not** symmetric under $h\leftrightarrow h'$, equivalently $X\leftrightarrow Y$. By the Jacobi identity, with $H=g^{-1}h$,
     $$F(X,Y;h,h')-F(X,Y;h',h)=-\sum_i\eta_i\,\mathrm{tr}\big([R(X,e_i),R(Y,e_i)]\,[H',H]\big).$$
     This is generically nonzero. For constant curvature, $\sum_i\eta_i[R(X,e_i),R(Y,e_i)]$ is $\pm(2-n)$ times the endomorphism $X\wedge Y$, the sign depending on the convention for $X\wedge Y$.
     `xcheck §X5` checks the identity and that the difference is nonzero, at random data in five signatures.
  3. $C_2=\sum_{a,b,q}\eta_qF(e_a,e_b;\theta^a\odot\theta^q,\theta^b\odot\theta^q)$ uses $h\neq h'$ and $X\neq Y$, so it contains the part that the leaf condition does not constrain.
     - Let $C_2'$ be the same sum with $h,h'$ swapped. Then exactly
       $$C_2-C_2'=\tfrac{n+2}2|\mathrm{Ric}|^2-\tfrac{n+2}4|R|^2 .$$
       By hand: the antisymmetric part reduces to $\sum_{ab}\eta_a\eta_b\,g(e_a,D_{ab}e_b)=\tfrac12|R|^2-|\mathrm{Ric}|^2$, with $D_{ab}=\sum_i\eta_i[R(e_a,e_i),R(e_b,e_i)]$.
     - `xcheck §X5` finds $C_2\neq C_2'$ in all five cases, e.g. (1,3): $C_2=121/2$, $C_2'=553/2$.
  4. Hence "$C_2$ is linear in $F$, so $F\equiv0\Rightarrow C_2=0$" is not a valid inference, and step 3 of Corollary L2 is not justified as written.
  5. The code does not test this step. `J5d_leaf.py` §L1 verifies the identities for $C_1$ and $C_2$ as defined, which are correct. Its §L4 check only evaluates $112(c_H/c_V)^2>0$.
  6. The conclusion survives.
     - The trace that the leaf condition does constrain is
       $$C_2^{\rm sym}:=\tfrac12(C_2+C_2')=\sum_{a,b,q}\eta_q\,\tfrac12\big[Q_{e_a,e_b}(h_{aq}+h_{bq})-Q_{e_a,e_b}(h_{aq})-Q_{e_a,e_b}(h_{bq})\big],\qquad h_{aq}=\theta^a\odot\theta^q .$$
     - It satisfies, for every algebraic curvature tensor,
       $$C_2^{\rm sym}=\tfrac{n+8}8|R|^2+\tfrac{n+4}4|\mathrm{Ric}|^2,\qquad 8(n+2)\,C_2^{\rm sym}-(n+8)\,C_1=2(n+2)(n+4)\,|\mathrm{Ric}|^2 .$$
       `xcheck §X5` checks this exactly at $n=3$ (1,2), $n=4$ (4,0), (1,3), (2,2), and $n=5$ (2,3). Its $C_2^{\rm sym}$ is computed from values of the quadratic form only.
     - At $n=4$, $4C_2^{\rm sym}-C_1=8|\mathrm{Ric}|^2$. For $R=W+\tfrac\Lambda3R_1$ the traces are $C_1=6|W|^2+16\Lambda^2$ and $C_2^{\rm sym}=\tfrac32|W|^2+12\Lambda^2$, so $4C_2^{\rm sym}-C_1=32\Lambda^2$ (`xcheck §X5`, three signatures).
     - So $F\equiv0$ gives $C_1=C_2^{\rm sym}=0$, hence $|R|^2=|\mathrm{Ric}|^2=0$ and $\Lambda_g=0$. Corollary L2 holds.
     - A posteriori, $C_2=0$ also holds, so the printed identities ($8C_2-C_1=28|\mathrm{Ric}|^2$ and so on) are true. Only the inference is wrong.
     - "$\Lambda^2$ is in the span of the coefficients of $F$" is also true: output §L3 certifies it directly from $F(X,X;h,h)$ in (1,3), (2,2) and (4,0), and (3,1) follows by $g\to-g$, which sends $F\to-F$. But the explicit combination offered, $8C_2-C_1$, is not one of these.
  7. Independent support already in the note:
     - Proposition L3 for Lorentzian and definite bases (a single quadratic evaluation; valid);
     - the L4 certificates, which use only $F(X,X;h,h)$ (valid).
- *Fix:*
  - Define $C_2$ with the symmetrised polarisation, or replace it by $C_2^{\rm sym}$.
  - State $C_2^{\rm sym}=\tfrac{n+8}8|R|^2+\tfrac{n+4}4|\mathrm{Ric}|^2$, and $4C_2^{\rm sym}-C_1=8|\mathrm{Ric}|^2=32\Lambda^2$ at $n=4$.
  - Change the ratio remark to "$6:16$ and $\tfrac32:12$".
  - Keep the unsymmetrised $C_2$ identity only as a side remark.
  - Carry the corrected constants into J5(d), phase-1, the J5d yaml and the `J5d_leaf.py` labels.
  - Add a check in `J5d_leaf.py` that the combination used is built from values of the quadratic form.
  - The committed proof step was invalid although its conclusion stands, so an in-place correction note and a `reports/CORRECTIONS.md` entry are appropriate. The entry is the coordinator's.
