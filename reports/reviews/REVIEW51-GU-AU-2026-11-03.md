## Universal vertical formula

The geodesic extension in AU1 is sufficient for the curvature calculation. In a coordinate trivialization normal at the chosen base point, every fibre has the same coordinate metric $c_VG_\lambda$. Extend a local fibre-geodesic field independently of the base coordinates. Then $\nabla_UU=0$ on a neighbourhood, and $[U,X^H]=0$ along the entire selected fibre. This justifies both the omitted first curvature term and differentiation of the connection identity in the vertical direction. A bracket chosen to vanish at only one point would not suffice.

Koszul gives $L_U=S_U+K_U$, with $S_U=y^{-1}U/2$ self-adjoint and $K_U$ skew-adjoint for the actual horizontal metric $c_Hy$. In the projectable frame the mixed operator is $-\partial_UL_U-L_U^2$. Along a fibre geodesic, $y^{-1}U$ is constant. Thus the derivative of its trace vanishes, $\operatorname{tr}(S_UK_U)=0$ and $\operatorname{tr}K_U^2=-\|K_U\|^2$. Adding F1's intrinsic fibre Ricci gives precisely AU1. No Riemannian-submersion assumption on Z1 is used.

The correction is a Gram form of the map into $\mathfrak{so}(H)$, hence positive semidefinite with the stated rank bound. The trace of each base curvature endomorphism is zero, which makes its action on $y$ tracefree and gives $K_E=0$. Ordered horizontal entries supply the Hilbert–Schmidt norm without an extra factor of one half.
