weight, the full transverse Jacobian, the positive graph Gram
identity and the Cholesky scaling. The same-center growth is a
tensor identity in a common real frame. The compression
determinant and explicit transverse coordinate bound control

at 24 generic positive matrices agree with the norm and scaling

uses the exact formulas.

with normalized surface measure. A small-strip estimate gives
decay for bounded densities, and the uniform square-integrable
majorant extends the decay to the stated center marginals.
Schur orthogonality fixes the value and the uniform lift constant.
Compact local holomorphic bounds justify analytic dependence of
the integrals.

entire-multiplier terms, then uses decay of the averaged terms on
the BW ray. It depends on the full equation's positive constant
norm and the BV receiver on the limiting triangular value.
