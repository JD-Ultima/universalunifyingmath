# Internal review of zero electric Weyl curvature

November 2, 2026. Review of `formalization/geometric-unity/EE-zero-electric-weyl.md`, the fresh driver, rank helper, rational JSON certificates and recorded output. EE1–EE6 are valid. This is internal review, not external peer review or a Lean formalization.

## Intrinsic normal form

The geometric condition is at one point of a smooth Riemannian four-metric: nonzero rank-one Ricci tensor $\rho p^\flat\otimes p^\flat$ and $W(p,u,p,v)=0$ on $p^\perp$. The mixed Weyl tensor is otherwise unrestricted. It does not impose the condition throughout a neighbourhood.

I checked the magnetic parametrization beyond diagonal examples. With

$$B_{ij}=\tfrac12\epsilon_{jkl}W_{0ikl},$$

the off-diagonal Weyl Ricci contractions are the three differences $B_{23}-B_{32}$, $B_{31}-B_{13}$ and $B_{12}-B_{21}$. They force symmetry, while Bianchi forces trace zero. The reconstruction $W_{0ikl}=\epsilon_{jkl}B_{ij}$ recovers every mixed component. The general nine-entry symbolic check uses these identities directly.

Since the electric tensor is zero, the spatial three-dimensional curvature tensor has zero Ricci tensor and is zero. This uses the invertible three-dimensional curvature-to-Ricci map, whose exact matrix determinant is checked as $-2$. Diagonalizing the real symmetric tracefree magnetic matrix by an oriented perpendicular frame gives precisely the two-parameter normal form, not an additional restriction on the mixed part. Both signs of the nonzero Ricci eigenvalue are retained by normalization with $|\rho|$.

## Polynomial compatibility

The hand total-degree bounds are valid for this mixed curvature family: coefficient degree at most two, RHS degree at most three, with parameter-independent denominators. The six and ten triangular nodes are unisolvent with determinants of absolute values 4 and 576. Thus the entry reconstructions are exact before compatibility determinants are computed. The zero cubic RHS coefficients are calculated within that bound, rather than assumed.

At each of the three specific positive diagonal fibres, the selected rows have one common metric combination of $n$ and $\xi_{13}$ as their only unknown combinations. Sparse coefficient identities and the four additional coefficient-node checks are verified there. No sparsity statement at arbitrary diagonal fibres is needed.

For each Ricci sign the three compatibility numerators generate the unit ideal over $\mathbb Q[a,b]$. This is a genuine common-zero elimination, giving a finite three-configuration cover without numerical root tests or a rotated fourth configuration.

## Rank at those same fibres

The symmetry is the even-sign subgroup. An odd coordinate reflection reverses the mixed Weyl tensor, so the full independent sign group used in CC would be inappropriate here. The helper correctly identifies complementary characters and obtains one 12-column block, three off-diagonal six-column blocks and four horizontal three-column blocks.

The rational certificates establish polynomial identities against the fresh six-node matrices. Constant left inverses give rank 12 and rank six in the first four blocks. Each horizontal block has a constant rank-two output; its nonzero universal kernel vector bounds its rank by two, so the rank is exactly two. Polynomial support checks exclude off-block couplings in the baseline subsystem. The total rank is therefore

$$12+3\cdot6+4\cdot2=38,$$

and the four independent sparse columns $K$ are its entire kernel at every parameter and every stated compatibility fibre.

The completion check is explicitly real. Each four-row restriction is a negative diagonal matrix with positive rational scaling, multiplied by a positive constant plus a positive semidefinite quadratic form in $(a,b)$. The helper verifies symmetry, both nonnegative diagonal entries and the nonnegative determinant of each two-dimensional quadratic matrix, together with the strictly positive constant and prefactors. These conditions prove strict positivity for all real parameters. Every displayed completion is therefore invertible on $K$, giving coefficient rank 42 at the same fibre that supplies incompatibility.

The complex unit-ideal calculation does not assert that these real-positive completion polynomials have no complex zeros. The note and helper maintain this distinction correctly.

## Higher jets and local scope

The DD3/BB3 support argument is independent of the fixed quadratic curvature. Cubic changes have three-horizontal/one-off-diagonal-vertical curvature support; diagonal-vertical gradients eliminate the missing components. This fixes the 722-row subsystem and the selected coefficient rows. Simultaneous base inversion fixes the quadratic metric and reverses cubic coefficients; the selected RHS has even parity and affine cubic dependence. The quartic principal-jet gradient cancellation supplies the remaining RHS invariance.

Actual higher jets need not have the baseline even-sign symmetries. The subsystem entries are unchanged by the support proof. In a completion row, the only cubic change is in $\xi_O$, which vanishes on $K$; quartics change no coefficient. Thus the same-witness rank and compatibility conclusions survive every cubic, quartic and smooth zero-four-jet remainder.

At each fixed actual metric, augmented rank 43 gives an actual nonzero minor at a positive coordinate fibre matrix. Rational fibre dependence excludes any fibre-open consistent patch. That fixed minor persists over a sufficiently small base neighbourhood, where nonzero curvature can also be retained. The U5 constant-piece/dense-variable-patch argument then applies. The zero-electric condition is needed only at the initial point, and no uniform neighbourhood is asserted.

The full-field conclusion retains compact $(14,0)$ and $\mathfrak u(128)$, Z1 at $\lambda=1/5$, positive $c_H,c_V$, invariant odd field, slaved vector even field, one fixed reconstructed Shiab member with $(x,y)\ne(0,0)$, $\kappa\ne0$ and $\det M\ne0$.
