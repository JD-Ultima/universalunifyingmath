**R6-2 (gap). J4(c)'s image condition is proved only at leading order. The exact statement, and an exact $\det H$ for the whole family, follow from a block factorisation.**
- *Location:*
  - J4(c), l. 146: "Near $t=u=0$ it holds … If $\det H_0=0$, both columns of $H_0$ are proportional to $(x,y)$";
  - Cor. J2.1(b), (c), and Answers 1 ("$\det\propto(xz\pm yw)^2$" to leading order);
  - J3(b), (c) ("If $\det H\ne0$ (e.g. $\circledast_\sharp$)").
- *What is missing:*
  1. The argument for $\det H_0=0$ is about $H_0$ only. The image of $H(t,u)$ could tilt away from $(x,y)$ at higher order, so the step does not prove the claim.
  2. Exact statement: $\ell_E$ vanishes on the $A,B$ columns and $S$ on the $A,B$ rows, so $H=-\ell_{CD}\,(M^{-1})_{CD}\,S_{CD}$. Two consequences:
     - $\mathrm{im}\,H\subset\mathrm{im}\,\ell_E$, and every column of $\ell_E$ satisfies $\det[\ell_{\cdot k},(x,y)]\propto xz\pm yw$. So whenever $xz\pm yw=0$, $\mathrm{im}\,H(t,u)\subset\mathrm{span}(x,y)$ exactly (`xcheck §X4`).
     - By Jacobi's complementary-minor identity, $\det(M^{-1})_{CD}=\det M_{(A,B)}/\det M=\kappa^2/\det M$. Hence, exactly, for every member and every $(t,u)$ with $\det M\ne0$:
       $$\det H=\frac{576^2\,(xz\pm yw)^2\,\kappa^2}{\det M}.$$
       This is checked at random rational points in both signatures (`xcheck §X3`). It contains $\det H_\sharp$ as a special case.
  3. Consequently, J3(b) and J3(c) ($d\tau=d\upsilon=0$, so $T_e=0$, so I7(a)/(b)) hold for **every** member with $xz\pm yw\ne0$, not only for Shiab#.
- *Fix:* replace the leading-order argument in J4(c) by item 2, add the exact $\det H$ to Cor. J2.1, and widen J3(b)/(c) accordingly.

**R6-6 (presentation). The justification of the invariant-theory step in (J).**
