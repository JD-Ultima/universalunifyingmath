## Mathematical checkpoints

- **Full grade one:** canonical trace-free projection, even valence14 and
  SO5→O5 extension permit the same classical pairing theorem as BI. The
  valence cases leave exactly four polarized contractions. Each is symmetric
  in the output arguments: `tr(A[S,R])=0` and the crossed trace is cyclic.
  Thus there is no additional antisymmetric component hidden by diagonal
  calibration. This is the hand proof; the ordered-entry control is a
  convention check rather than its universal justification.
- **Gram and normalization:** the35 triple blocks retain all ordered
  multiplicities. The nonsingular four-row calibration, nonzero nonscalar
  multipliers and BI's explicit105-dimensional operator decomposition prove
  the full scalar-Gram equivalence. The scalar trace gives
  `q=-(26/21)||B||²g`, so the entire grade-one equation also requires
  `||B||²=21ell/26`. This sign permits an amplitude if a nonzero Gram solution
  exists; it supplies no general no-go. The zonal rank14 Gram prevents
  reusing the cubic rank contradiction.
- **Constrained isometry:** the creation adjoint has tensor coefficients
  `1/3` and `-2/21`, equivalently polynomial correction `-2r²(Sx)_i/7`.
  Its trace and Frobenius pairing are correct, and norm polarization gives
  `sum a_i*a_i=I_W`. Canonical input-pair-trace-free coefficients make the
  self-adjointness equality exact. The permutation `(24)(35)` together with
  the existing S3×S2 yields `(14)` and then all S5, proving full tensor
  symmetry, not merely symmetry modulo traces. Normalization gives norm²14.
  The target dimension30 leaves a genuine isometry-feasibility problem.
- **Complete coordinate space:** the seven degree-five monomial orbit sums
  exhaust the coordinate-S5 invariants. Their exact Laplacian rank3 gives
  precisely the four displayed harmonic basis vectors. Tensor coefficients
  are stored as `120*T`; norm and all traces consistently remove that
  constant factor. Every one of625 Gram slots is evaluated. Primitive rows
  are normalized only by nonzero rational constants, and their complete
  set is exactly the four independent printed coefficient rows. All four
  quadratic equations in the proof agree with these rows, including every
  mixed amplitude term.
- **Projective coverage:** every nonzero real or complex amplitude vector
  has a highest nonzero coordinate. Homogeneous rescaling sets it to1 and
  higher coordinates to0; these four strata lose no case. Rational linear
  algebra constructs multiplier degrees at most3,1,0,0 respectively, then
  exact expansion verifies `sum h_i*f_i=1` on each stratum. This is a unit
  ideal certificate, not sampled roots, numerical optimization or a division
  by an unknown polynomial. It excludes the entire four-dimensional space.
- **Complete simplex line:** all seven restricted degree-five orbit sums
  reduce to `span(p5,p2*p3)`. The exact Laplacians and nonzero harmonic
  generator establish the one-dimensional kernel. The ambient tensor
  annihilates the all-ones direction and has every pair trace zero, so the
  projected six-coordinate contractions equal those on Euclidean E. Norm
  `10/33`, projector trace14, all1296 entries and discrepancy `-10/7623`
  have the correct normalization. Scaling/rotation cannot repair it.
- **Even groups and fields:** alternating parts have Vandermonde degrees10
  and15, greater than5. Thus the A5/A6 fixed polynomial spaces equal the
  respective S5/S6 spaces; only the even groups are needed inside SO5.
  The equivariant harmonic-to-hook intertwiners transfer those classifications.
  BA's arbitrary-hook derivative has grade7, so variable amplitudes cannot
  cancel the grade-one obstruction. Dilation and homothety give the common
  positive `cV^-7` moment/norm factor and actual positive baseline. Metadata
  correctly limits the exclusions to these spaces and their SO5 conjugates.

## Limits of acceptance

The criterion concerns the full grade-one equation, not the full GU system.
General real quintic Gram feasibility, higher quadratic grades, covariant
closure and field reconstruction remain open. The exact fixed-space
classifications do not exclude arbitrary W40 values, mixed harmonic modules
or all curvature-kernel fields. The setting remains flat positive Z1,
lambda1/5, all positive constant scales and the declared real compact WA
pure branch with nonzero constant kappa. Other parameters, curved bases,
coincident sectors and other real forms/operators remain outside scope.
No old main, suite, git operation, physical claim or literature-priority
claim is part of this review.
