# Internal review of the hook-line catalogue and graded obstruction

Scope: V1–V3 in [V](../../formalization/geometric-unity/V-hook-intersections.md), following T5. This is a separate internal review of the proof and executable certificate, not external peer review or an independent curvature implementation.

## Assessment

The finite catalogue, necessary norms and specified grade-five obstruction are valid under the stated hypotheses. They reduce the remaining hook-line problem without classifying its full solutions. In particular, the explicit rejected element is a counterexample to sufficiency of the scalar trace tests.

The assumptions remain signature (14,0), compact u(128), real fields, the fixed parallel W_A operator, kappa nonzero, det M nonzero, and a connected domain on one of the six stated values of t/kappa. V3 additionally fixes u=0 and the first, pure H2Gamma kernel branch.

## Catalogue and norms

R6's factorizations split each exterior block into two two-by-two pairs. With a=t/kappa and r=(u/kappa)^2, each pair has determinant proportional to (1+da)(1+ea)-pq*r. This directly gives the four rational table entries. Their positivity and pairwise distinctions imply one additional exterior copy at each signed nonzero intersection. The other exterior block, Lambda7 and the vector block are regular there. All six hook ratios are distinct, so exactly one hook copy occurs. Homogeneity allows kappa to have either sign.

Together with T5, this leaves nine possible constant odd pairs on each fixed line: zero and eight signed intersections. It does not prove existence at any of them.

For V2, the Gamma trace and Lk=0 give
\[
 \langle k_\Lambda,Dk_\Lambda\rangle=28u^2(1+208a).
\]
The two component equations and self-adjointness imply
\(A\|k_1\|^2=B\|k_2\|^2\). Consequently
\[
 \|k_\Lambda\|^2
 =14u^2(1+208a)(1/A+1/B)
 =14\kappa^2(1+208a)(A+B)/(pq).
\]
This derivation cancels nonzero kappa without assuming it positive. Orthogonality to the hook summand and T2 give V2.2. Its nonnegative hook norm is an additional necessary condition, not a solution criterion.

## Full odd equation on the first branch

The derivative term cannot be discarded. Levi-Civita differentiation preserves Clifford grade, and the W_A symbol maps grade 12 to grade 11. The quadratic terms have grades 1 and 5. This follows directly by writing a grade-12 element as a grade-2 element times Gamma: Gamma commutes with even grades, anticommutes with odd grades, and squares to -1. The grade-12 commutator becomes a grade-2 commutator; the adjoint-gradient term reduces to a grade-3/grade-2 anticommutator. That anticommutator has grades 1 and 5. The revised V3 proof uses these identities explicitly.

Thus the three graded equations are necessary and together reproduce the odd equation on this branch. A grade-five value cannot be cancelled by curvature, the invariant background, or any derivative jet. The displayed element has norm squared 4 and nonzero Q5=4i*w(E1234), so every nonzero real multiple fails. This is an algebraic obstruction for those elements, not all hook tensors.

The flat Z1 control is geometrically admissible: cV=36414/30599 and cH=2*cV are positive, lambda=1/5, and F7 gives the stated Einstein tensor. Both invariant traces vanish pointwise for the displayed local-frame field; its grade-five equation still fails. No parallel-field assumption is needed.
