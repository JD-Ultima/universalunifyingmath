**R13-1 (error). Q15: "$\det M\neq0$ at one of the two roots for every $a$" fails for $a^2=\tfrac{125}{576}$ at the threshold, where the two roots coincide.**
- *Location:*
  - Q note, Q15 statement (l. 219) and proof (l. 221);
  - Q16(b) (l. 229, "these exist iff $c_V\kappa^2\ge9360(x'^2+y'^2)$", under "Assume (H1)");
  - N note, N.4 (l. 261, "with $\det M\neq0$ at one root always");
  - yaml `formalization_status` step (10) (l. 26).
- *What is wrong:*
  - With $\omega:=9360(x'^2+y'^2)/(c_V\kappa^2)$, the roots of $312T^2+\kappa T+\omega\kappa^2/1248=0$ are real iff $\omega\le1$. At $\omega=1$ they coincide: $T=-\kappa/624$.
  - On $u=0$, $\det M/\kappa^2=(\kappa+224T)(\kappa+544T)-147456a^2T^2$. At $T=-\kappa/624$ this is $\kappa^2(32000-147456a^2)/389376$, which vanishes for $a^2=\tfrac{125}{576}$, i.e. $a=5\sqrt5/24$ (`xcheck §X8`, check [37]).
  - So for that member, at the threshold, the only real root lies on $\det M=0$. The proof's "both roots on $\det M=0$ would need $a^2=-\tfrac{115}{144}$" covers two distinct roots only.
  - The resultant in ω (`xcheck §X8`, [36]; up to a constant factor the polynomial of the note's output §Q9, [31]) has:
    - leading coefficient $\propto(144A-119)^2\ge0$;
    - constant term $\propto-(2304A+319)<0$;
    - value $\propto(576A-125)^2\ge0$ at $\omega=1$.

    So for every real $a$ there is **exactly one** $\omega_*\in(0,1]$, i.e. one value $c_V\kappa^2/(x'^2+y'^2)=9360/\omega_*\ge9360$, at which a root lies on $\det M=0$. $\omega_*=1$ iff $a^2=\tfrac{125}{576}$. "At most two values" is true but weaker than this.
  - Here $a$ is $w'/x'$ of the rotated member; for a general member $a^2=(x'w'-y'z')^2/(x'^2+y'^2)^2$ (`xcheck §X8`, [35]).
  - Consequences:
    - Under (H1) ($\det M\neq0$), Q16(b)'s "these exist iff $c_V\kappa^2\ge9360(x'^2+y'^2)$" needs the strict inequality for $a^2=\tfrac{125}{576}$.
    - The threshold configuration still solves $\Upsilon=0$ on $Y$: with $T_e=0$ the even equation $M\vec A=-S(dt,du)=0$ holds whatever $\det M$ is. It is only outside (H1).
    - So the summaries' "exist iff $c_V\kappa^2\ge9360(x'^2+y'^2)$", read as existence of solutions of $\Upsilon=0$, stay correct.
