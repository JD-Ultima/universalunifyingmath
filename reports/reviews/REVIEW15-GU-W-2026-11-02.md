W1–W4 are valid under their stated hypotheses. The quadratic coefficients remain those of U and the cubic coefficients remain zero. Rank-one Ricci and zero Weyl are conditions **at the origin**; neither is asserted throughout a neighbourhood.

## Why every quartic coefficient is covered

**Jet order.** A quartic metric perturbation first changes the base connection at cubic order. Since the connection vanishes at the origin, the observer horizontal–vertical metric block changes at cubic base order and its horizontal–horizontal block at order four. The vertical block is fixed. Thus the observer 2-jet and the relaxed coefficient matrix are unchanged. Its 3-jet, and consequently the relaxed RHS, depend affinely on the quartic coefficients. Higher remainders have no effect on these jets. Mixed fibre derivatives cannot reduce the base order.

**Gradient cancellation.** At a diagonal fibre matrix, let $A=y_{33}$ and write $\mathcal H_j$ for the cubic change in $h_{jA}$. The diagonal Christoffel trace identity gives

$$\mathcal H_j=\partial_jF,\qquad
F=-\frac{c_V}{d_3}\left(Q_{4;33}-\lambda\operatorname{tr}Q_4\right).$$

This includes off-diagonal quartic coefficients. In the principal variation of $\nabla\mathrm{Ric}$, the terms containing a vertical derivative vanish at the origin. The remaining contraction is

$$\delta(\nabla_i\mathrm{Ric})_{iA}
=\frac12\sum_{c=0}^3h^{cc}
\left(\partial_c\partial_i^2\mathcal H_c-
\partial_c^2\partial_i\mathcal H_i\right)=0.$$

Each summand cancels by commuting fourth derivatives of $F$. Also $\delta(\nabla_A\mathrm{Ric})_{ii}=0$, since the lower observer jets are fixed along the entire fibre. The inverse metric used to raise the output index is fixed. Therefore all three selected RHS rows are unchanged for arbitrary $Q_4$. Other RHS rows can change; preserving the entire RHS is unnecessary and would be false.

## Scaling and the local consequence

Dilation sends $(\rho,Q_4)$ to $(\rho s^2,s^4Q_4)$. Rotations preserve the unrestricted quartic class. U's reviewed lift, fibre scaling and observer homothety therefore reduce every nonzero $\rho$ and positive metric scales to the two signed representatives, with normalized quartic tensor $Q_4/\rho^2$. The smooth remainder retains its zero four-jet under these maps.

At each fixed metric, coefficient rank 42 and the row contradiction give augmented rank 43. A genuinely nonzero augmented 43-minor can therefore be fixed and continued to nearby base points. The integer row weights themselves need not continue to annihilate nearby coefficient matrices. W4 correctly uses continuity of the minor, followed by the rational fibre argument and U5's constant-piece/dense-variable-patch reduction. No uniform neighbourhood over quartic coefficients or remainders is required.
