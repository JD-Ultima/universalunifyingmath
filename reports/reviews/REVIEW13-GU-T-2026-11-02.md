# Review of the singular-sector residual identity

November 2, 2026. Internal cross-review of `formalization/geometric-unity/T-singular-residual.md`, its exact script and recorded output, following commit `a338747`. This review checks T1–T5 under their stated reconstruction; it is not external peer review.

## Assessment

T1–T5 are valid as stated. The residual identity extends R10's uniqueness result to a fixed **nonconstant** invariant odd field whose slaved baseline already solves the equation. It also supplies a necessary sign condition when that baseline has a nonzero residual. The applications exclude two specified singular lines under a curvature-trace condition and exclude nonconstant motion along the six positive hook lines. They do not classify all singular branches.

The hypotheses are essential: signature (14,0), compact gauge real form $\mathfrak u(128)$, real fields, the parallel four-parameter Shiab family, $\kappa\ne0$ and $\det M\ne0$. No positivity conclusion for signature (7,7) follows.

## Proof checks

**T1: derivatives and mixed terms.** In the dualized convention, $\mathcal D_\xi={*}^{-1}\circledast(\xi\wedge\cdot)$ is skew for the positive pairing $-\operatorname{tr}$. R4 places $\mathcal D_\xi T_o$ in the vector isotype $\mathcal V$. The complementary bundle $\mathcal W$ is parallel for the Levi–Civita spin connection, so every $\nabla^0_a k$ lies in $\mathcal W$. Applying the skew-symbol identity separately to those derivatives gives

$$\langle T_o,{*}^{-1}\circledast(d_0k)\rangle=0.$$

This does not differentiate $T_o$ or require the varying kernel of $L_{T_o}$ to be parallel. It uses only the parallel isotypic splitting. Cubic symmetry and preservation of $\mathcal V$ by $L_{T_o}$ also give $\langle T_o,2\mathcal B(v,k)\rangle=0$.

**T2–T3: sign and uniqueness.** Pairing the even kernel equation with $k$ gives $\langle T_o,Q(k)\rangle=-\kappa\|k\|^2/2$. Therefore a full solution satisfies

$$\frac{\langle T_o,E_v\rangle}{\kappa}=\frac12\|k\|^2\ge0.$$

The sign and factor of two are correct for either sign of the nonzero real parameter $\kappa$. If the scalar residual vanishes, $k=0$; the baseline must still satisfy every remaining odd equation. In particular, an already solved baseline has a unique even field for that fixed $T_o$. This is not uniqueness of the odd field or an existence theorem for a residual of the permitted sign.

**T4: the two negative singular lines.** Direct expansion gives

$$tF_1+uF_{13}=(\kappa+312t)(t^2+u^2).$$

The compact real-form phases give norm 14 to both invariant copies and zero cross pairing. Thus T4.1 has the stated curvature term and coefficient. For $a=t/\kappa<-1/312$ and $\operatorname{tr}_{g_Y}G^Y\ge0$, its quotient by $\kappa$ is strictly negative. Both $a=-1/176$ and $-1/208$ satisfy this condition; both factors of $\det M$ are strictly negative there, so their product is positive. The curvature-trace hypothesis remains an additional geometric assumption.

**T5: the positive hook lines.** On a pure-hook kernel, independence of the hook blocks from $u$ gives $\mathcal B(k,i\Phi^1\Gamma)=0$. The zero $du$ source gives the corresponding zero derivative contraction. Hence the odd Γ trace requires $u(\kappa+208t)=0$.

For each of the six positive hook ratios, $\kappa+208t\ne0$ and the $\Lambda^7$ blocks are invertible. The two remaining non-vector determinants are genuine quartics in $u$, with leading coefficients $126877696/9$ and $1638400$. Their real roots form a finite set $F$. Away from $F$, the trace forces $u=0$, so $u(U)\subset F\cup\{0\}$. Continuity and connectedness prove that $u$ is constant. No submersion or analytic-continuation argument is needed.

## Computation and limits

A separate symbolic check, importing no T implementation, verified the T4 expansion and all six rational hook ratios. Substitution into R6's factorizations gives four distinct positive squared values of $u/\kappa$ across the two quartics on each line: eight possible nonzero real values before the $\det M\ne0$ restriction. This confirms finiteness; it does not establish a solution at any intersection.

The command `python -X utf8 computation/geometric-unity/T_singular_residual.py` completed successfully in about four seconds. Its deterministic output matched the recorded `.out` file. Those tests check real-form phases, the cubic and current identities, R10's zero-residual control, both signs of $\kappa$ on the two T4 lines, and the T5 coefficients. The test diagonal $G^Y$ is algebraic coefficient data, not a constructed observer metric.

No integration, boundary condition, manifold compactness or analyticity is used. Constant $u=0$ pure-hook solutions and constant nonzero-$u$ coincident-kernel cases remain open, as do general nonconstant singular curves, $\det M=0$, non-invariant odd fields and indefinite real forms. The pointwise proofs, rather than the finite tests alone, support T1–T5.
