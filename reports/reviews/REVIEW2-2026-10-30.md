## 2. Independent verification of the three new mathematical claims

**(a) GU Prop. A7** (A-chimeric-signature.md:56–75; note Prop. `prop:spin`).

1. *Extension class.* $K=S(O(7)\times O(7))$. $H^*(BK;\mathbb Z_2)=\mathbb Z_2[w_i(E_\pm)]/(w_1(E_+)+w_1(E_-))$, so $H^2$ has basis $w_2(E_+),w_2(E_-),w^2$.
   - Restrict to $\langle r_{ab}\rangle$. For $a,b$ both in $E_+$: $w(E_+)\mapsto(1+u)^2$, so the restrictions of $(w_2(E_+),w_2(E_-),w^2)$ are $(u^2,0,0)$. For both in $E_-$: $(0,u^2,0)$. For a mixed pair: $w(E_\pm)\mapsto1+u$, giving $(0,0,u^2)$.
   - These are unit vectors, so restriction is injective on $H^2$.
   - The lift of $r_{ab}$ is $\gamma_a\gamma_b$, with $(\gamma_a\gamma_b)^2=-\gamma_a^2\gamma_b^2$: this is $-1$ for a same-sign pair and $+1$ for a mixed pair. Hence $o=w_2(E_+)+w_2(E_-)$.
   - Sanity check: Spin(1,1) → SO(1,1) splits over $\{\pm1\}$, so every SO(1,1) bundle lifts. The criterion does not depend on the Clifford sign convention, because $\mathrm{Cl}^0(p,q)\cong\mathrm{Cl}^0(q,p)$.
2. *Decomposition.* For base (1,3) and $\lambda>\tfrac14$: $G_\lambda$ is positive on $\mathrm{Sym}^2_0\Sigma^*$ (5) and on the complement of $y$ in the trace plane (1); it is negative on $\ell^*\otimes\Sigma^*$ (3) and on $\mathbb Ry$ (1). Check: on the trace plane, $\det=3-12\lambda<0$ and $G(y,y)=4-16\lambda<0$.
3. *Splitting principle, by hand.* With $\sigma_i$ the elementary symmetric functions of the Chern roots of $\Sigma$:
   - $w(\mathrm{Sym}^2_0\Sigma)=1+(\sigma_1^2+\sigma_2)+\dots$;
   - $w_2(C_-)=t^2+t\sigma_1+\sigma_1^2$;
   - hence $o=\sigma_2+t\sigma_1+t^2=w_2(X)+t^2$, and $w_1(C_\pm)=t$.

   This agrees with `A_spin_obstruction.out` K2.
4. *Fibers.* On a fiber, $\ell$ restricted to $\mathbb{RP}^2\subset\mathbb{RP}^3$ is the tautological line, so $t^2\neq0$. On $Y'\simeq S(TX)$, Gysin gives injectivity of $\pi^*$ in degrees ≤ 3.
5. *Extra check, not in the repo.* For a base of signature (2,2), $w_1(C_\pm)=w_1(N)$, and $o=\pi^*w_2(X)+w_1(N)^2$, which is nonzero in $H^2(\mathrm{Gr}_2(\mathbb R^4);\mathbb Z_2)$ (`gu/spin22.py`). So "no Spin(7,7) structure over Met(X)" also holds for (2,2). The note does not prove this (N5).

**(b) Howard: no finite ground certificate for the 12 subsets** (arithmetic.md:86; note §Formal verification).
- *The model.* $\phi(q)=q-1$ for $q\le0$ and $-1/(1+q)$ for $q>0$ is an order isomorphism $\mathbb Q\to\mathbb Q_{<0}$. The map Φ acts on exponents, so it preserves well-ordered supports and leading coefficients; it is order-preserving and ℝ-linear. Then $a\otimes b=\Phi^{-1}(\Phi a\,\Phi b)$ is bi-additive, associative and commutative. Its valuation is $v(a\otimes b)=\phi^{-1}(\phi(v_a)+\phi(v_b))<\min(v_a,v_b)$, and its leading coefficient is positive, so $a\otimes b\gg a,b$. INC and BAL therefore hold on all of $K$.
- *Numerical sanity check.* An exact test on 400 random finite-support series found no failure of LD, RD, AS, CM, INC or BAL (`howard/lattice_and_model.out`).
- *Transfer.* Ackermannise the finite ground set; the resulting existential sentence has real parameters and holds in $K\supseteq\mathbb R$, so it holds in ℝ by model completeness of RCF. The argument is sound.

**(c) CT counts.** See CT-1, CT-2 and CT-5 in §1. All were reproduced from first principles (`ct/mutations_in_space.out`).
