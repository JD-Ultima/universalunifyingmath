# Internal review of the exterior-conic trace equations

November 2, 2026. Cross-review of `formalization/geometric-unity/AA-conic-traces.md`, its exact script, output and metric record. AA1–AA3 are valid on their stated domains. This is internal review, not external peer review.

## Domain and slaving

The result concerns a smooth arc of one of four real $W_A$ exterior conics, away from every other non-vector singular factor. The extra kernel is therefore one paired $\Lambda^3$ or $\Lambda^5$ copy. Intersections, hook additions and larger kernels are not included. The hypotheses retain compact $(14,0)$, real fields, fixed parallel $W_A$, constant $\kappa\ne0$ and both vector-invertibility conditions

$$\delta=1+224a-3328f(a)\ne0,\qquad
1+544a-3328f(a)\ne0.$$

The variables are $a=t/\kappa$, $w=u/\kappa$, with $w^2=f(a)=AB/P$. No assumption that $a$ is a submersion is needed. A smooth nonconstant field can have critical points; these are retained in the scalar equations.

The two sourced vector equations are

$$ (1+224a)C-16wB_0=24\,da,\qquad B_0-208wC=0.$$

Thus $C=24\,da/\delta$ and $B_0=208wC$, including for negative $\kappa$. The factors of $\kappa$ cancel before this formula; replacing $da$ by $dt$ without its factor $1/\kappa$ would be incorrect. The second determinant factor remains an assumption even though it does not appear in this sourced block.

## The traces and residual normalization

The compact vector traces give $1456|C|^2$ and $-208\langle B_0,C\rangle$. With $|C|^2=576|da|^2/\delta^2$, the latter is

$$-208^2\cdot576\,w|da|^2/\delta^2
=-24920064\,w|da|^2/\delta^2.$$

The derivative traces are $312\operatorname{div}C$ and zero. The complementary isotypic bundle is parallel, so the non-vector derivative currents vanish even when the kernel and its field vary along the conic. There is no assumption that $\nabla k$ lies in the varying kernel. Cubic symmetry and vector-isotype preservation likewise kill the two mixed **traces**, not the mixed tensor itself.

For the paired kernel, self-adjointness gives $AN_1=BN_2$. Its two quadratic traces are $HN$ and $-PwN/h$. Here $h$ is nonzero everywhere on the real conic: $h=0$ together with $AB\ge0$ would require $A=B=0$, impossible when $d\ne e$. These norm-weight formulas remain valid at either zero-$w$ endpoint.

The baseline residual in AA1.3 follows by taking $a$ times the baseline $\Phi^1$ trace plus $w$ times its Gamma trace. I checked the derivative coefficient and the gradient numerator after substituting $\delta'=224-3328f'$. The factor $N/2$ in T2 is also consistent with the identity

$$aH-\frac{Pf}{h}=-\frac12.$$

Consequently the residual identity after Gamma elimination is $a$ times the substituted first trace. It is not legitimate to recover that first trace by dividing by $a$ at $a=0$. AA correctly derives it directly.

## Division, reparametrization and signs

The algebraic norm formula uses division by $w$ and applies only where $u\ne0$. Its substitution into the first trace gives the asserted scalar equation. Combining the gradient terms yields exactly $-3744\delta'/\delta^2$, which accounts for the coefficient $-\delta'/(2\delta)$ after normalization. Positivity of the kernel norm gives the stated right-branch gradient bound; it does not prove existence of a corresponding tensor field.

On each left branch, the displayed endpoints give $a<-1/312$, $1+208a<0$, $f'<0$, $h<0$ and $\delta<0$. Hence $F>0$ and $\mathscr R>0$. With the stated convention $\Delta=\operatorname{div}\operatorname{grad}$ and $z'=(-\delta)^{-1/2}$,

$$z''=-\frac{\delta'}{2\delta}z',\qquad
\Delta z=\frac{\sqrt{-\delta}}{7488}
\left(\mathcal G+\kappa^2\mathscr R\right)>0$$

when $\mathcal G\ge0$. This is a strict subharmonicity statement and excludes an interior local maximum of $a=t/\kappa$. All sign conclusions involve $\kappa^2$, so they hold for either sign of $\kappa$. They do not assert the same extremum condition for $t$ when $\kappa<0$.

The endpoint argument correctly avoids the divided Gamma equation. If a smooth field on a left branch reaches $w=0$, its $a=\alpha$ is a local maximum. Thus $da=0$ and $\Delta a\le0$. In the undivided first trace, $\delta<0$ makes $7488\Delta a/\delta\ge0$, while $F>0$, $H>0$, $N\ge0$ and $\mathcal G\ge0$. The term $14\kappa^2F$ makes the sum strictly positive. No endpoint norm condition is borrowed from the $w\ne0$ formula or assumed by continuity.
