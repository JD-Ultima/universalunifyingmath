# Internal review of DD: one mixed Weyl direction

Scope: DD1–DD4 in `formalization/geometric-unity/DD-mixed-weyl.md`, the fresh driver, rank helper and rational certificates. This is an independent internal mathematical review, not external peer review or a second curvature implementation.

## Assessment

DD1–DD4 are valid within the declared simultaneous frame orbit. The result covers both signs of nonzero rank-one Ricci curvature, every real amplitude of the specified mixed Weyl tensor, arbitrary higher smooth metric jets and positive observer scales at lambda=1/5. It does not classify general mixed Weyl tensors or the Ricci-flat nonflat case.

The full Upsilon conclusion retains all of S.0: compact (14,0), gauge algebra u(128), invariant odd field, slaved vector even field, fixed reconstructed Shiab member with `(x,y)!=(0,0)`, nonzero kappa and `det M != 0`. The explicit nonzero-member condition clarifies the existing S.0 reference; no theorem narrowing was required.

## Compatibility and rank at the same fibres

The tensor built from the symmetric cross terms (01,23) and (02,13) has the stated entries `(1,-1,0)`, Bianchi and zero Ricci contraction. All its nonzero entries have four distinct indices, so its electric and sectional components vanish. Its nonzero mixed component remains outside CC's vanishing-mixed hypothesis under frame changes fixing the Ricci line.

The coefficient/RHS degree bounds are established from the metric jets before interpolation. At each of the two stated positive diagonal fibres, the complete three coefficient rows have precisely the common metric combination and xi13 coefficient. The two exact compatibility numerators have a unit Bezout identity for each Ricci sign. Thus at least one of these finite configurations is incompatible at every amplitude; no root of an individual certificate is treated as a solution.

Only the even-sign subgroup fixes this auxiliary quadratic tensor. Complementary off-diagonal characters therefore merge into three six-column blocks. The helper correctly uses that group, rather than imposing CC's larger independent-sign symmetry. Constant rational left inverses give ranks 12 and 6; four horizontal blocks have constant rank-two outputs. The polynomial identity `MK=0` supplies four independent kernel columns, so the rank lower bound `12+18+8=38` is also the exact rank. The constant invertible J7 restriction completes that same subsystem to rank 42 at both compatibility fibres, for both signs and all amplitudes.

## Why the higher-jet transfer includes mixed curvature

I checked DD3 directly against the jet argument in BB3. The quadratic curvature need not be diagonal.

- In normal base coordinates, the observer metric and first jet are fixed when cubic coefficients vary. Only the mixed horizontal/vertical second metric derivatives change. Thus the cubic Riemann variation has three horizontal slots and one vertical slot. The diagonal-vertical gradient identity removes that slot when it is diagonal. At a diagonal fibre, the inverse metric preserves the diagonal/off-diagonal vertical split, so the corresponding raised curvature and Ricci supports are unchanged. This fixes the 722 coefficient rows and the three selected complete rows for any fixed quadratic tensor.
- The RHS is affine in the cubic and quartic tensors. Cubic dependence is removed by total base inversion `x -> -x`: this fixes every quadratic metric, including the mixed tensor, while reversing cubics. Each selected RHS component has two horizontal slots and even parity. This is distinct from the smaller even-sign group used for the auxiliary rank calculation.
- A quartic variation leaves the lower observer jets fixed. Its mixed third metric variation is a gradient in each diagonal-vertical slot. Commuting fourth derivatives cancels the selected principal covariant-Ricci term; the companion vertical derivative vanishes by base order. Neither the fixed mixed curvature nor the actual cubic tensor changes that cancellation. A zero-four-jet remainder enters neither required observer jet.
- In each completion row `(i,7,7)`, the cubic Riemann support is impossible. The only variable Ricci coefficient multiplies xi7, which vanishes on every column of K. Hence the invertible restriction survives arbitrary higher jets even when those jets break every auxiliary reflection symmetry.

These are universal support and parity arguments. The finite controls below check conventions and do not supply their quantifiers.

## Local consequence and retained limits

The fixed-43-minor step in DD4 is essential and correctly stated. For each actual metric, a nonzero augmented minor at its positive coordinate fibre witness persists over a base neighbourhood. Nonzero Ricci permits that neighbourhood also to retain nonzero base curvature. The fibre rational-minor argument then applies over every base point there. U5 excludes constant-field Einstein pieces through S5 and obtains a forbidden relaxed-system patch from Q5 on the dense variable-field part. No sparse three-row relation at arbitrary fibres is continued.

The neighbourhood depends on the actual metric; no uniform radius is claimed. The full-field exclusion concerns S.0's slaved vector sector, with its real-form and regularity assumptions. General non-vector singular solutions, other Weyl orbits and physical predictions are not consequences of DD.
