## Exact lift and every curvature channel

Constant base-metric scaling preserves the Levi-Civita connection. Consequently its connection one-form is $\Gamma_\varepsilon(z)=\varepsilon\Gamma_g(\varepsilon z)$.

For the covariant fibre metric, the lift is exactly
$$
\Psi_\varepsilon(z,h)=(\varepsilon z,\varepsilon^{-2}h).
$$
The fibre covariant differential pulls back to $\varepsilon^{-2}D_{g_\varepsilon}h$. The horizontal powers cancel, and the affine fibre metric satisfies $G_{\lambda,ah}(aU,aV)=G_{\lambda,h}(U,V)$. Thus AX4b is an identity of the **full** observer metrics, with unchanged $c_H,c_V,\lambda$.

At $(0,I)$ their tangent inner product is fixed. The clarified AX4d wording explicitly differentiates the difference from the constant-base metric: the horizontal term $c_Hh$ and vertical block $c_VG_{\lambda,h}$ are independent of $\varepsilon$. The remaining horizontal correction is quadratic in the connection, and the mixed block is linear in it. Differentiating these corrections through total order two, including all fibre and mixed derivatives, introduces only smooth functions of $h,h^{-1}$ and connection jets that tend to zero. The displayed powers $\varepsilon^{|\beta|+1}$ are correct. Smooth base geometry supplies the required finite jets; base analyticity is unnecessary.

Curvature therefore converges in one fixed orthonormal tangent frame to the entire flat-base tensor. This includes horizontal, mixed and vertical channels, rather than only Ricci or intrinsic fibre curvature. The differential of the lift sends the frame to an orthonormal frame at the finite radial point $(p,qg_p)$, with horizontal factor $q^{-1/2}$ and vertical factor $q$, where $q=\varepsilon^{-2}$. No varying-frame limit is hidden.
