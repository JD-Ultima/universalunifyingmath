### α Letter, F-CT-4 E (mechanical trial-factor space)

**R4-CT-1 (gap). The density, and hence "lower than B" and "robust", depend on truncating the space at depth 2.**
- *Location:*
  - `mechanical-trial-factor.md`, Result in brief 5 ("The F-CT-4 conclusion is robust to replacing B by the mechanical space") and §7, first bullet ("Here the density is lower than B's").
  - `audit.md` F-CT-4 E, fifth bullet ("So the conclusion of B is robust …"), and Limits ("the mechanical space of E gives a lower one (31.6 against 103.9 per unit)").
  - The CT-G10 note in `gaps/chain-theory-alpha.md` ("robust to replacing the hand-declared space").
  - `CT-F04-mechanical.yaml`: `result` ("Yes."), `proof_status` and `confidence` ("both declared spaces give expected chance hits of order 1e-6").
- *Problem:*
  - The space is all singles and all pairs. Its density of distinct values in $(120,155)$ is set mainly by that depth: 1.09 per unit for singles and 31.6 for singles plus pairs, a factor 29.
  - B is a complete product over 8 parameters, so it has no comparable truncation. The comparison "31.6 against 103.9" therefore compares a depth-2 neighbourhood of the file with a full grid. It is not a property of mechanically generated spaces.
  - §6 and §7 say that triples are not evaluated. They do not say that the density is still growing with depth, which is what decides whether "lower than B" is a stable statement.
- *Evidence:* x-out [6] uses seeded samples of 12 000 pairs and 12 000 triples, with pairwise-disjoint sites as in the generator's pairs, both evaluated by the transcription.

  | | distinct values | distinct in $(120,155)$ | seen once / twice | Chao1 estimate for the whole depth-$k$ space |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | pairs | 1911 | 288 | 162 / 40 | 616 (true value 1105, out §4) |
  | triples | 3191 | 389 | 251 / 42 | 1139 |

  - Chao1 underestimates at these sampling fractions: it gives 616 for pairs, whose true count is 1105.
  - Even so, its depth-3 estimate already exceeds the full depth-2 count. With the depth-2 calibration ratio it is about 2000 in-band values, or about 58 per unit, giving $\approx2.4\times10^{-6}$ expected chance values.
  - The ratio does not transfer exactly. The depth-3 sampling fraction is about 270 times smaller, and Chao1 underestimates more as the fraction falls, so 2000 is a low estimate.
  - Lean 4.35.0 agrees with the transcription on 4 of the sampled triples (x-out [7]).
  - Space sizes before the disjointness rule are $\binom{818}{3}=9.1\times10^7$, $\binom{818}{4}=1.9\times10^{10}$ and $\binom{818}{5}=3.0\times10^{12}$. The last two bracket the "$2.5\times10^{11}$ mutants for an $O(1)$ chance hit" of Result 5.
- *Effect:*
  - Order of magnitude: through depth 3 the expected number of chance values stays of order $10^{-6}$ (between $1.3$ and a few $\times10^{-6}$), so the F-CT-4 order of magnitude is not overturned.
  - The direction of the comparison with B is established only at depth 2. The depth-3 estimate, a low one, is about 58 per unit, still below B's 103.9, but nothing bounds depth 4. The word "robust" without a depth is therefore not established.
- *Fix:*
  - Write "at mutation depth ≤ 2" wherever the mechanical density or its comparison with B is stated.
  - Add the depth dependence, as singles 1.09 and pairs 31.6 per unit, with the triple sample's estimate as a lower indication.
  - Replace "lower one (31.6 against 103.9)" by "at depth ≤ 2 a lower one; the density grows with depth".
  - In the yaml, `result` should not open with an unqualified "Yes".

**R4-CT-6 (presentation). "Mutants for an $O(1)$ chance hit" mixes redundancy with density.**
- *Location:* `mechanical-trial-factor.md`, Result in brief 5 and the last row of the §4 table; the yaml `result`.
- *Problem:*
