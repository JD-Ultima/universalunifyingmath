# Review108. The H4 fixed-space covariance and sparse hook restrictions

**Date** 2026-11-14. **Verdict** Accept the stated covariance and support results.
[CW](../../formalization/geometric-unity/CW-h4-fixed-positive-moment.md).
[CX](../../formalization/geometric-unity/CX-hook-sparse-support.md).

The independent CW exhaustive run regenerated all 363 integer
evaluations, both 68-dimensional basis ranks, and all 227
original Clifford control coordinates. Integer perturbation
bounds give the exact Neumann defect below \(1/20\), the
covariance correction below \(1/64\), and the final positive
margin \(15/64\). Character averaging and the exterior trace
identity make the 227 actual coordinates complete. The
conclusion is recorded as a covariance relaxation.

For CX, the trace counts prove the minimum real external
ranks. The graph proof tests the diagonal and off-diagonal
scalarity coefficients after reduction modulo two. It lifts
the complete homogeneous degree, which avoids assuming a
single modular ideal membership automatically lifts. The
verifier confirms seven exact cyclic systems and twelve graph
controls. A separate rank-four H4 construction was rebuilt
over \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{22})\), verifying all scalarity and top
equations and a nonzero grade-five output.
