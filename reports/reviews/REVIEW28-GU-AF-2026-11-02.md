## Assessment and assumptions

AF1–AF3 are valid as stated. On the single Lambda3 contraction/exterior-Gamma kernel branch, a full solution has no point with nonzero u. On a connected domain remaining in that branch, its odd field is therefore constant at one of the two stated endpoints. ## Conversion and universal tensor coefficients

The right-Gamma convention is consistent throughout. Converting `i*u*(kappa+208t)*Phi1Gamma` by right multiplication gives the negative scalar stated in AF1 because `Gamma^2=-1`.

For a unit simple three-form, the complete symbolic quadratic output has the stated eigenvalues `-88rs/3` on its three-plane and `40rs` on the perpendicular eleven-plane. Their trace is `352rs`, the negative of the unconverted Gamma-partner trace. This agrees with the previously established cubic-pairing convention. SO(14) transitivity on unit simple forms and quadratic homogeneity extend that complete tensor output to every real simple form, with arbitrary symbolic weights. No assertion on arbitrary three-forms is inferred from the calibration.

The aligned-vector formula is universal on the actual slaved family because `B0=208wC`. Every nonzero aligned pair is obtained by rotating its unit direction and varying the two symbolic amplitudes; the zero-vector case follows directly. Its converted trace is `43264w|C|^2`, consistent with the negative of AA1's partner trace after this conversion.

At a zero of omega, the stress tensor can be written as `rs` times the Gram matrix of its contractions. For a simple form this is `nu*P`, and it vanishes at zero. Thus the pointwise tensor equation requires no smooth global choice of a three-plane frame.

## Derivative and mixed terms

The note's equivariant symmetric-vector-jet argument is valid: the only symmetric output maps are a multiple of the symmetric derivative matrix and a multiple of its trace times the metric. The complete coordinate outputs force both coefficients to vanish.

There is also a direct grade check for the actual vector part. The WA anticommutator on P2, of grade two, has only grades one and five. It annihilates P14 entirely because Gamma anticommutes with the Clifford triple. Neither supplies grade thirteen, including derivatives of its variable covector coefficient.

For the kernel derivative, AA4's grade-thirteen term becomes a contraction lift of a two-form after right-Gamma conversion. Its coefficient matrix is antisymmetric, including every weight derivative. For the mixed vector/kernel term, `V tensor Lambda3` decomposes as `Lambda4 + Lambda2 + H3`, with no scalar or symmetric-traceless two-tensor summand. It therefore has no symmetric converted output. These are statements about arbitrary covariant jets; no derivative of a chosen simple-form factorization or projector is taken.

These checks account for all terms in the symmetric channel and establish AF1 even when the baseline itself is unsolved.

## Rank contradiction and endpoints

The right side of AF1 has rank at most four, while a nonzero scalar identity has rank fourteen. Its scalar coefficient must therefore vanish. The remaining equality compares one rank-at-most-one tensor with a nonzero multiple of a rank-three projector. Consequently nu is zero.

At a hypothetical nonzero-w point, both real chart weights are nonzero by the two kernel equations. Positivity gives omega=0, and the tensor equality gives C=0. The scalar equation then forces `a=-1/208`. The exact value `f(a)=-3/14872` contradicts real `w^2=f(a)`. This argument is pointwise and uses kappa only through its nonzero square; it includes either sign of kappa.

After w vanishes everywhere, the conic leaves exactly `a=-3/112` and `a=-1/400`. Connectedness and continuity force one constant value. This does not classify the residual fields at either endpoint or solutions that involve other kernels.
