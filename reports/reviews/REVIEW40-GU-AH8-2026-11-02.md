## The spectral argument

The positive observer metric makes the five-form contraction Gram self-adjoint, with `0 <= S <= N I`, `tr S=5N` and `N>0`. AH6 gives

    Ric_hat = xi I - ell S,
    xi = b/6 + (2q1+5ell)N/12,   b>0.

For `ell>0`, each nonpositive Ricci eigenvalue consumes at least `xi/ell` of the trace budget. Thus its nonpositive index satisfies

    m <= 5ell N/xi < 60ell/(2q1+5ell).

For `ell<0`, use `S'=N I-S`, `tr S'=9N`, and

    Ric_hat = zeta I - |ell| S',
    zeta = b/6 + (2q1-7ell)N/12 > 0.

This gives `m <= 9|ell|N/zeta < 108|ell|/(2q1-7ell)`. The strict final inequalities follow from `b>0`, even when zero Ricci eigenvalues occur. The rational limits in endpoint order are `8,72/7,200/19,216/19`, so the integer caps are **7,10,10,11**. The first forbidden counts exceed their trace budgets by `b/8`, `5N/8+11b/384`, `9N/40+11b/640`, and `N/2+b/16`, respectively. All are strictly positive.

The argument is universal positive linear algebra, with no eigenvalue sampling or continuation of an inequality. It does not assert that five-form Grams or observer curvatures attain these caps.

## Geometric consequences and limits

At the actual local-parallel set defined in S2, F4 and S3 give a negative-definite ten-dimensional vertical block, zero mixed block, and horizontal tensor

    Ric_g - (cH/c)y,   c=4cV(1-4lambda)>0.

Pointwise curvature annihilation alone does not define this set. The local-parallel hypothesis is needed to apply those blocks.

The first endpoint cannot meet this set. At the middle two, the horizontal block must be strictly positive definite: a horizontal zero direction would exceed the nonpositive-index cap. The last allows at most one nonpositive horizontal direction. If the base Ricci rank is at most two, its kernel has dimension at least two; the horizontal tensor is strictly negative on that kernel. Together with the vertical block this gives at least twelve negative directions, contradicting every endpoint cap.

It neither turns an isolated endpoint of a moving odd field into such a domain nor excludes domains avoiding the local-parallel set. Remaining derivative equations, varying Gram spectra and actual endpoint existence are unresolved.
