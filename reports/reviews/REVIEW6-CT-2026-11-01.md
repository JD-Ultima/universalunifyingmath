## Continuation and limits

The useful immediate continuation is to keep these bounded checks in the current verification suite. `xcheck_round_ct5.py` and `records_uniform.py` run without external sources; adding `--upstream` to the latter also checks source anchors. The mechanical cross-check needs the pinned Lean file and runs its optional Lean stage only when `LEAN` is supplied.

The remaining CT questions should retain their present grades:

- **CT-G03/G04:** an axiom-level justification for matching closure and the stated proper-algebra condition remains an input question. Repeating the already proved trace classification does not derive it.
- **CT-G09:** OD plus ID reproduces the plain-frame records, and the covariant construction additionally chooses the qubit exchange sign. The finite classification does not show these rules follow from the Letter's axiom.
- **CT-G10:** mutation depth greater than two and choices outside the Lean text remain outside the exhaustive family. A new depth-3 investigation should declare its operator set and resource bound before reporting results, distinguish samples from exhaustive counts, and retain the illustrative-model qualification of density reference counts.
- **CT-G01/G02/G08:** the weight attribution and amplitude-level reading remain separate from the record-space and conjugation results. None of these reruns supplies a physical derivation of the candidate value.

No active mathematical conclusion, numerical value, claim grade or historical review was changed by this continuation review.
