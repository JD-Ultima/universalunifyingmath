signs and the complete inverse Gram apply in the nonorthogonal
weight coordinates. The two alternating and contraction maps and the

it gives a lower bound on rational rank. The original internal upper-bound
review independently rebuilt a rational split basis, metric inverse,
and curvature entries using native fractions, then checked one
maximal minor in each of the 185 blocks by reverse elimination. The
public verifier instead computes all modular ranks from scratch, with
no frozen pivots or matrix entries.

two Cartan actions, two extremal root actions and within-type

assembly. Both build the root actions from the 5-by-5 matrices.

vector-frame weights, so the specified simple roots annihilate highest
vectors with the displayed dominant labels. Complete reducibility
and the Weyl dimensions give the matching lower bound, using all 18
copies. A fresh run reproduced the rank, dimension, all multiplicities
and the sign witness in about 43 seconds.
