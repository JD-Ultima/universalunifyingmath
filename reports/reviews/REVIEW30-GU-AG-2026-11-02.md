## A separate derivative proof

AA4's formula for the other copies is not used here. For a Clifford triple and an even homogeneous monomial, the anticommutator retains the products with an even number of common indices. Grade three times grade four therefore gives only grades seven and three. Grade three times grade twelve has at least one common index in dimension fourteen; its only allowed even intersection has size two, giving grade eleven.

Consequently the full $W_A$ derivative of this field lies in grades three, seven and eleven. The connection preserves Clifford grade and the parallel volume element, so this conclusion includes arbitrary covariant form jets and weight derivatives. It excludes grades five, nine and thirteen without discarding the derivative tensor.

The nonzero Cartan controls are relevant: they demonstrate derivative components surviving in the permitted grades. They cannot be replaced by an exterior-derivative/codifferential formula borrowed from AA4, and remain part of the endpoint field equations.

## Symmetric Gamma equation and rank

For a unit simple form, the symmetric part of the grade-thirteen output, right-multiplied by $\Gamma$ and divided by $i$, has eigenvalues $-88rs/3$ and $40rs$ on dimensions three and eleven. Equivariance and quadratic homogeneity therefore give

$$40\nu I-\frac{208}{3}\nu P,\qquad \nu=rs|\omega|^2.$$

The non-coordinate simple-form control checks the full stress formula. At zero, only the product $\nu P$ is used and is defined as zero; no smooth global three-plane or factorization is assumed.

The odd field and slaved vector sector are unchanged from AF1. Their symmetric contribution, together with the new simple-kernel tensor, yields exactly

$$[-\kappa^2w(1+208a)+3328w|C|^2+40\nu]I
=3328wC\otimes C+(208/3)\nu P.$$

The kernel derivative has no original grade-thirteen component by the preceding proof. The mixed term has no symmetric converted component by $V\otimes\Lambda^3=\Lambda^4\oplus\Lambda^2\oplus H_3$. The curvature term has original grade one and is absent from this selected channel. No curvature-trace sign is needed.

The right side has rank at most four, so its scalar multiple of the fourteen-dimensional identity must be zero. If $\nu\ne0$, the resulting equality would equate a rank-three projector multiple with a tensor of rank at most one. Hence $\nu=0$. At a putative $w\ne0$ point both weights are nonzero; positivity gives $\omega=0$, and the tensor equality gives $C=0$. This uses no alignment between $C$ and the three-plane.

The remaining scalar equation forces $a=-1/208$, but the other conic has

$$f(-1/208)=-1/43264<0.$$

This contradicts real $w^2=f(a)$. The argument is pointwise and works for either sign of $\kappa$.
