## Intrinsic scope and normalization

The common-kernel condition is essential. Relative to the nonzero Ricci eigenline, the real symmetric tracefree electric and magnetic Weyl matrices must commute **and share a nonzero kernel vector**. Arbitrary commuting pairs need not have that property.

Normalize such a vector as the third perpendicular frame vector. Symmetry preserves its orthogonal two-plane, on which the two restrictions are commuting symmetric tracefree matrices. Simultaneous diagonalization gives

$$E=\operatorname{diag}(\alpha,-\alpha,0),\qquad
B=\operatorname{diag}(\beta,-\beta,0).$$

An eigenvector sign change supplies an oriented frame without altering either diagonal matrix. The argument includes either matrix zero. Conversely the two stated curvature profiles give exactly those matrices. I checked the mixed-profile sign: $H_{0123}=1$ and $H_{0231}=-1$ give $B=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0)$, not its negative. The spatial curvature is determined by $\mathrm{Ric}_{\mathrm{spatial}}=-E$ in dimension three, and $W_{0ikl}=\epsilon_{jkl}B_{ij}$ recovers the mixed part. Thus the stated intrinsic condition is precisely this simultaneous-frame orbit.

The normal-coordinate quadratic coefficient has Ricci $\rho p^\flat\otimes p^\flat$, with $\rho\ne0$. U's reviewed naturality and scaling maps preserve the normalized amplitudes $a=\alpha/|\rho|$, $b=\beta/|\rho|$, while allowing arbitrary normalized cubic and quartic jets. Positive $c_H,c_V$ are normalized by the same maps. Positivity requires only a sufficiently small base neighbourhood; no common radius over all higher jets is asserted.

## Polynomial compatibility at finite witnesses

The degree bounds are established before interpolation. On the auxiliary quadratic metric the observer metric and inverse at the point are parameter independent; its relevant derivative degrees imply total degree at most two for the coefficient matrix and at most three for the RHS. The denominators are independent of $(a,b)$. This remains a hand jet argument, rather than a conclusion drawn from the computed nodes.

The six triangular coefficient nodes and ten RHS nodes have nonzero Vandermonde determinants of absolute values $4$ and $576$. The driver freshly assembles all sixty exact systems: ten nodes at three positive diagonal fibres, for each Ricci sign. It does not import raw node data or use an off-repository cache.

At each stated fibre the selected three rows have only the common metric combination and $\xi_{13}$ as unknown combinations. Unwanted coefficients vanish identically by the prior bound and six-node reconstruction; four additional nodes separately check the selected coefficient polynomials. RHS cubic coefficients vanish as an exact computed result, not as an assumed degree reduction. The resulting three-row determinants have fixed rational denominators, so taking primitive numerators preserves their zero sets.

For each sign, exact computation over $\mathbb Q[a,b]$ gives Gröbner basis $[1]$ for the three compatibility quartics. Thus they have no simultaneous root. This proves that at least one of the **three specified fibres** is inconsistent at every parameter pair. No sparsity identity at an untested diagonal fibre, density matching, or extrapolation from the one-parameter $a=b$ pencil is used.

## Rank at those same witnesses

The rank argument is independent of compatibility. The auxiliary quadratic family is fixed by the even coordinate-sign subgroup. Odd sign flips reverse the mixed tensor, so the full independent-reflection decomposition would be inappropriate here. The correctly merged characters give a twelve-column trivial block, three six-column off-diagonal blocks, and four three-column horizontal blocks.

The JSON contains rational constant row combinations, not sampled ranks. The helper checks their identities against all six fresh coefficient matrices in every signed-layout case. Unisolvence then makes them polynomial identities. Constant left inverses give ranks $12,6,6,6$; constant rank-two outputs give the four horizontal lower bounds. The four independent columns

$$K_i=e_{28+i}+\frac{24d_i}{5}e_{14+i}$$

are identically in the invariant subsystem kernel, supplying the matching upper bound. Hence its rank is exactly $38$ and its kernel exactly their span, at all parameters and at each of the three finite witnesses.

The four $J_7$ rows restrict to a constant diagonal invertible matrix on this kernel. All three displayed restrictions, for both Ricci signs, are checked at the six coefficient nodes. This proves coefficient rank $42$ at the same witnesses used for compatibility, without parameter exceptions, rank sampling or a separate determinant search.

DD3's support argument is independent of the chosen fixed quadratic curvature. It therefore applies with both electric and mixed parts present. Cubic perturbations leave the 722 invariant coefficient rows fixed. Diagonal-vertical gradient cancellation fixes the selected coefficient rows; affine cubic dependence and simultaneous base inversion eliminate their RHS variation. In each completion row, the possible cubic change multiplies only $\xi_7$, which is zero on every $K_i$. Thus the invertible completion persists. The even-sign symmetry was used only for the auxiliary quadratic certificate; no such symmetry is imposed on the actual cubic or quartic terms.
