## Mathematical checkpoints

- The coefficient covector and all form indices are differentiated. The
  external divergence D is a q-form and differs from form-slot co-closure.
  The argument is tensorial in a normal frame and does not assume a parallel,
  invariant or coordinate-supported hook field.
- Direct even-form Clifford expansion retains both q+1 and q-3 terms. With
  Hbc=nabla_b Bc-nabla_c Bb, the entire lower sum is exactly
  `2 i_a sum_b i_b nabla_b(Ctr B)`, so differentiated contraction kills it.
  The first two wedge terms give `-2 e^a wedge D`; contracting differentiated
  Alt gives the third as `2(d_nabla B)_a`. Thus BA1 is the full derivative,
  rather than a selected-grade identity.
- Its contraction is `-2(13-q)D`, namely -22,-14,-6. Conversely contracting
  closure already gives D=0. This proves both directions of the derivative
  equivalence for q=2,6,10.
- The spanning argument covers all jets. For an external-absent coordinate,
  each union of q+1 axes has q+1 coefficients and one alternating relation;
  D differences span that kernel. For an external-present coordinate, each
  remainder of q-1 axes has 15-q coefficients and one contraction relation;
  R differences span it. Their disjoint dimensions give the complete hook.
  A jet uses at most q+2<=12 axes, so an unused-axis reflection repairs the
  permutation determinant without changing the input. SO14 covariance then
  preserves right Gamma and supplies universal coverage.

  actual degree12,8,4. The phase i therefore gives the declared compact real

  Q/i Hermitian. Odd Hermitian real Clifford grades are 1 mod4. Consequently
  they cannot cancel derivative grades11,7,3. The curvature and u=0 constant
  baseline have grade1, and R/V leave just the indicated pure even kernel.
- The third row is correctly distinguished: `B10=-C4 Gamma` gives
  `iB10 Gamma=iC4`. Parallel Hodge star exchanges the hook traces and gives
  `d_nabla B10=0` exactly when `delta_nabla C4=0`; it does not give closure
  of C4. The coefficient-curvature integrability condition follows from
