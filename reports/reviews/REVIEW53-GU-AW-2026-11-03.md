## Normal moments and real Clifford conventions

The canonical change `eta=r*omega`, `tau=s/r` retains the full derivative
`nabla eta=r*nabla omega+dr tensor omega`. Substituting it into AS3 and dividing
the common nonzero factor gives the stated canonical coefficients. It does
not set either weight derivative to zero. On a regular patch, `rho=|C|>0`,
and both weights are nonzero by the nonzero-w opposite-conic equations.

For `eta=e0 wedge phi+psi`, set `U=phi4`, `V=psi5 J` and `Gamma=e0 J`.
Positive Clifford multiplication gives `J^2=1`, `Gamma^2=-1`, and
`e0 psi Gamma=-V`. Thus AS3's axial square equation gives `F8=zeta*rho*V`
with the displayed positive sign. The opposite copy is
`r*w(omega)+s*c(omega)Gamma` with real weights: its grades six and ten are
anti-Hermitian in the compact convention. No extra factor of i or inverse
Gamma is introduced in the moment calculation.

The ambient Cl14 scalar coefficient is a cyclic normalized matrix trace.
It must not be replaced by a single-chirality Cl13 trace, which can see the
odd-dimensional pseudoscalar. Grade orthogonality in the ambient algebra
gives `tr U=tr V=tr UV=0`, `tr U^2=m` and `tr V^2=ell`. Pairing the square
equation with U and V yields

    M30+M12=alpha*rho*m,
    M21+M03=zeta*rho*ell.

The normal sandwich is exactly

    Omega e0 Omega=e0(U^2-psi^2)+{U,psi}.

Its anticommutator is retained. Pairing its grade-five component with
`e0 U,psi` gives `M30-M12,2M12`; pairing its grade-nine component multiplied
on the RIGHT by Gamma gives `-2M21,M21-M03`. To check the axial sign directly,
cycle Gamma onto `e0 U`: `Gamma e0 U=-J U`. Both anticommutator terms then
contribute `-M21`. The terms with one remaining normal generator have zero
scalar coefficient. These identities use cyclicity and grade projection,
not commutation, simultaneous diagonalization or a finite spectrum bound.

The complete normal AS equation gives

    A(M30-M12)-2B M21+rho*p*m=0,
    2A M12+B(M21-M03)-rho*q*ell=0.

Substituting the two square traces makes the same quantity `2A M12+2B M21`
equal both `rho(A alpha+p)m` and `rho(B zeta+q)ell`. Division by positive rho
proves AW1. No form norm piece is divided away.

## Pointwise corollary and boundaries

AW5 follows from the smooth Clifford isotypic splitting in R2/R4. A nonzero
Lambda5 projection persists on a small neighbourhood. The even equation has
no source in that isotypic component, so its homogeneous block must be singular
throughout that neighbourhood. Every other singular factor, explicitly
including the OTHER Lambda5 factor, remains nonzero after shrinking. Hence
the same one of the two conics holds throughout the neighbourhood, all other
extra components vanish, and the vector part is slaved because `det M!=0`.

The two-by-two copy block has rank one on that one conic: it cannot be the
zero matrix for nonzero kappa, including at its endpoints. Local smooth real
kernel charts therefore give exactly the ansatz of AP or AW. A connected
smaller neighbourhood contradicts their domain exclusions. This argument
does not exclude an odd-field conic contact or crossing when the extra
Lambda5 projection is zero.
