- *Problem:*
  - The proof of sufficiency treats only $B=A$. For a general $B\supseteq A$ the statement fails.
  - Take $B=A\oplus\mathbb C(\mathbb 1+\sigma_x^{(q_1)})$ and, at slot $q_1$, $x=\sigma_x\otimes\sigma_x-\tfrac12(i\sigma_y\otimes\sigma_z-\sigma_z\otimes i\sigma_y)$. Then $m(x)=\mathbb 1+\sigma_x^{(q_1)}\in B$ and $m(\tau x)=\mathbb 1-\sigma_x^{(q_1)}\notin B$.

- *Fix:*

*Upper bound for anticommuting pairs at $d=3$.*
- Let $A$ be a traceless Hermitian $3\times3$ matrix with an anticommuting partner $B\neq0$. Then $B$ maps the $a$-eigenspace of $A$ to the $(-a)$-eigenspace, and $\ker A$ to itself. So $\mathrm{spec}\,A=\{a,-a,0\}$, and the traceless Hermitian anticommutant of $A$ is the 2-dimensional real space of off-diagonal Hermitian matrices on $S=E_a\oplus E_{-a}$.
- Hence a letter has at most two anticommuting partners in an orthogonal basis. If it has two, the three letters span $\mathfrak{su}(S)$ and anticommute pairwise.
- Two such triangles would need $\mathfrak{su}(S_1)\perp\mathfrak{su}(S_2)$ for 2-planes meeting in a line $\mathbb Cv$. But $\operatorname{tr}\big[(|v\rangle\langle v|-|u_1\rangle\langle u_1|)(|v\rangle\langle v|-|u_2\rangle\langle u_2|)\big]=1+|\langle u_1|u_2\rangle|^2>0$.
- So the graph is at most one triangle plus disjoint edges on 8 vertices: at most $3+2=5$ pairs. The Gell-Mann basis attains 5.
