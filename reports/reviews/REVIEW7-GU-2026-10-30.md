**The structure of (C1)$_0$ (referee's derivation).**
- Notation:
  - $E_{cd}\in\mathfrak{so}(\eta)$, $(E_{cd})^a{}_b=\delta^a_c\eta_{db}-\delta^a_d\eta_{cb}$; this is $R_1(e_c,e_d)$;
  - the curvature operator $\mathcal R$ is the linear map $E_{cd}\mapsto R_{cd}:=R(e_c,e_d)$;
  - $\mathrm{Cas}=\sum(kg^{-1})^{ab}K_a\otimes K_b$ for the trace form $kg$.
- REVIEW6-GU (`xcheck_round8` §X6a) has $\sum\epsilon_c\epsilon_dE_{cd}\otimes E_{cd}=-4\,\mathrm{Cas}$. Hence $B_R:=\sum\epsilon\epsilon R_{cd}\otimes R_{cd}=(\mathcal R\otimes\mathcal R)(-4\,\mathrm{Cas})=-4\,\mathcal R\,kg^{-1}\mathcal R^\top$.
- Pair symmetry of $R$ is self-adjointness of $\mathcal R$ for the trace form, so $B_R=-4\,\mathcal R^2\cdot\mathrm{Cas}$.
- $q$ is linear and injective on $\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathfrak{so}(\eta)$ (21-dimensional; the span certificate of L, rank 21, says the same), and $q(\mathrm{Cas})=-3c_0$. So $c_0+(c_V/c_H)^2q(B_R)=q\big((c_V/c_H)^2B_R-\tfrac13\mathrm{Cas}\big)$ vanishes iff $\mathcal R^2=-\tfrac1{12}(c_H/c_V)^2\,\mathrm{Id}$.
- Ricci-flat case:
  - For Ric = 0, $\mathcal R=W$ commutes with $*$ and satisfies $\mathrm{tr}\,W=0$ and $\mathrm{tr}\,W*=0$ (first Bianchi).
  - On $\Lambda^2\otimes\mathbb C=\Lambda^+\oplus\Lambda^-$, the eigenspaces of $*$ (eigenvalues $\pm1$ or $\pm i$, 3-dimensional each), these give $\mathrm{tr}\,W|_{\Lambda^\pm}=0$.
  - $W^2=k\,\mathrm{Id}$ with $k\neq0$ makes $W|_{\Lambda^\pm}$ diagonalisable with eigenvalues $\pm\sqrt k$, and three such numbers do not sum to 0.
  - This proves M5(c) over $\mathbb C$ in every signature, independently of the Gröbner computation.
  - It also explains the rank 12: $W^2$ consists of two symmetric $3\times3$ blocks.
- Real solutions:
