Keeping both is essential: imposing the larger full-sign invariance would lose orientation-dependent $SO(4)$ invariants. The 24 signed representatives have positive determinant and represent the permutation quotient by that normal even-sign subgroup.

Although these representatives need not form a subgroup by themselves, they act as the quotient on the even-sign fixed space. Averaging a coordinate orbit is therefore the finite-group invariant projection up to an irrelevant common factor. The action is monomial. Distinct coordinate orbits have disjoint support; a surviving orbit has a one-dimensional fixed space, while a sign-changing stabilizer makes its average zero. Exhausting the coordinates and checking each surviving average against every representative consequently proves both spanning and independence. This supplies complete finite invariant spaces of dimensions 80 and 216, not a selection of convenient examples.

The covector change from $E_{ii}$ to $D_1,D_2,D_3,I$ is applied to the external slot and every form slot, with the exterior signs.
