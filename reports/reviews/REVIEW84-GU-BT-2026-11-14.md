# Review84. Quadratic exclusion of the closed character-15 block

**Date** 2026-11-14. **Verdict** Accept BT1–BT3 on the declared
73-dimensional subspaces. [Proof](../../formalization/geometric-unity/BT-h6-triangular-character-obstruction.md)
and [verifier](../../computation/geometric-unity/BT_h6_an73_quadratic.py).

The computation uses all invariant Clifford coordinates allowed
by the sign character. The degree-one part is homogeneous
tracefree scalarity. Its diagonal trace was checked to vanish on
all monomial columns. Fourteen structural zeros at grade thirteen
were checked separately. The rank never uses the unadjusted stress
trace or a norm equation that would have a nonzero right-hand side.

The complete map was independently checked by a floating singular
value diagnostic and two exact modular eliminations with different
row orders and block widths. The exact eliminations establish
column rank 2,701 with nonzero block determinant products.
The public method reconstructs the rational basis and the entire
coefficient map directly. Its default mode checks 36 exact rational
Clifford receivers, including all four degrees, and ranks a
16-column subset. The BLAS implementation uses exactly
representable integer sums with a runtime integer guard.

The proof uses modular reduction only to establish a lower
bound on rank over characteristic zero. All column monomials
must then vanish at any common zero, so the algebraic argument
holds over the complex numbers. Axis reversal negates the transported
connection, preserves the hooks and takes character 15 to character
30. The full quadratic is equivariant, giving the second individual
exclusion. Sums of different characters remain open. External peer
review remains outstanding.
