keeps both five-blade amplitudes and both real chart weights symbolic and
compares every Clifford coefficient in all fourteen external slots. The six

at most ten axes; a reflection on an unused axis repairs a signed
permutation's determinant without changing the inputs. Thus the proof uses
SO(14), preserving Gamma, rather than assuming reflection invariance of a
single-Gamma expression. The diagonal case and quadratic polarization cover
every real five-form.

The gamma contractions separate square components by grade: four and six
in the first equation, eight and ten in the second. Their cross coefficient

`F4` and `F8` to vanish. When exactly one weight is zero, the remaining

After the square becomes scalar, right multiplication of the second

The resulting determinant is exactly

$$
\frac{32}{9}(35r^4+176r^2s^2+405s^4)>0
\qquad\text{for }(r,s)\ne(0,0).
$$

Both higher sandwich components therefore vanish. The normalizer step is
also valid: for positive norm `N`, conjugation by the real grade-five
element is an orthogonal involution. In its orthonormal eigenbasis, a
five-blade commutes precisely with its five included generators. Every
nonzero coefficient must consequently have the same support, the positive
eigenspace, so the form is simple. Zero norm gives the zero form by
positivity. Conversely zero and simple forms satisfy the displayed higher
identities. This argument does not cover complex weights or indefinite
null forms.
