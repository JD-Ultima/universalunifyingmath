**R3-CT-1 (gap). The two theorems do not reach every word length for $k_r$, $|C|$ or $\sqrt C$.**
- *Location:*

Nine length-3 non-product words already have the mixed-block ratio $+2$ of $K_L$, the very ratio that Theorem 4 forbids for products. They fail only on the other classes. Nothing in Lemma 3 or Theorem 4 prevents a longer word from compressing to $cK_L$.
- *Fix:* "Lemma 3 extends the $\sqrt{k_r}$, trace and value negatives to all lengths and all $\mathbb Q(i)$-linear combinations. Theorem 4 extends the $k_r$ negative to all products of factorizing letters and their twirls. Apply this in the three places, and in the yaml's `theorems` line if it is meant to summarise scope.
