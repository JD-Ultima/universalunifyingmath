# Internal review of Y: the zero-curvature quartic direction

Scope: Y1–Y2 and the exact certificate in `formalization/geometric-unity/Y-zero-curvature-quartic.md`. This is an independent internal review of the argument and supplied calculation, not external peer review or a second curvature implementation.

## Assessment

The origin-fibre obstruction is valid for the declared metric four-jet, every nonzero real beta, every unit direction and positive observer-metric scales. No mathematical correction or narrowing was needed. The note correctly stops before claiming base-neighbourhood persistence or a local full-field Upsilon exclusion.

The assumptions are important: the quadratic and cubic base jets vanish, the quartic term is exactly `beta*(p.x)^4*I`, and the smooth remainder has zero four-jet. The observer model is Z1 at lambda=1/5. Zero curvature at the origin does not mean flatness or Ricci-flatness on a neighbourhood.

## Jet dependence and the fibre rank

The W1 jet-order argument applies with the quadratic curvature term set to zero. A quartic base variation first changes the Levi-Civita connection at cubic order. In the observer metric, the mixed horizontal/vertical block first changes at base order three; the horizontal block changes only at order four or higher. Fibre differentiation does not reduce base order. Thus the observer two-jet, and hence the complete relaxed coefficient matrix, agrees with the flat model at every point of the origin fibre. The observer three-jet is affine in beta, and a zero-four-jet remainder does not enter it. With lower jets fixed, the third-metric-derivative contribution to the Ricci derivative is linear. Flat-base Z1 is Einstein at lambda=1/5, so the constant RHS is zero. This proves `b(beta,y)=beta*b(1,y)` for all beta; the three engine evaluations are controls of this identity rather than its proof.

The flat-model maps `(x,y) -> (B*x,B^-T*y*B^-1)` preserve both the horizontal metric and the vertical DeWitt pairing. They fix the base origin and act transitively on positive-definite fibre matrices. Covariance therefore transfers the exact coefficient rank 28 at the supplied witness to the entire positive fibre. This is a proof of constant rank on that fibre, not an assumption imported from one matrix calculation.

The positive homothety/fibre-scaling map has the stated direction: pulling back `alpha*h(cH,cV)` by `y -> q*y` gives horizontal scale `alpha*q*cH=2` and vertical scale `alpha*cV=3`. It leaves the base connection and beta unchanged. Rotations cover every unit p. No normalization of the magnitude or sign of beta is required.

## Exact certificates and reproduction

The three displayed sparse rows contain all their 42 coefficients. Their weights `(4,-3,2)` cancel the coefficients of `v^4`, `v^8` and `v^11` separately, leaving RHS `-3*beta`. The supplied augmented minor uses 29 distinct rows, the first 28 coefficient columns and the RHS column. Its determinant is

```text
beta*30517578125/13249474533898474022240256.
```

Only the final column changes with beta, so this scaling follows from determinant multilinearity as well as the beta=-2 control. The coefficient and augmented ranks at beta=1 are exactly 28 and 29.

The command

```text
python -X utf8 -I computation/geometric-unity/Y_zero_curvature_quartic.py
```

passed all 12 assertions in 1.7 seconds with Python 3.12 and SymPy 1.14.0. The sparse rows, ranks, exact determinant and affine controls match the recorded output. The script reuses W's point helper, U's relaxed-system assembly and the earlier reviewed `xcheck_round10` curvature engine. This rerun checks the executable certificate; the jet, covariance and determinant arguments above were checked separately by hand.

With coefficient rank 28 everywhere on the origin fibre, consistency on a fibre-open patch would force the displayed augmented 29-minor to vanish there. Its rational fibre dependence, after clearing denominators regular on positive-definite matrices, would then force its numerator polynomial to vanish identically. The nonzero witness contradicts that. The witness need not belong to the proposed patch.

## Limits retained

A nonzero augmented 29-minor does not preserve inconsistency under unrestricted perturbations when the coefficient rank may rise to 29 or more. Y therefore supplies no base neighbourhood of inconsistent fibres. It also does not exclude a field reduction whose line coefficient vanishes on the origin fibre: such a reduction need not produce a relaxed-system patch there with nonzero coefficient.

The result leaves general zero-curvature four-jets, arbitrary quartic tensors at zero quadratic curvature, and the Ricci-flat nonflat case unresolved. Its fibre obstruction is a useful boundary result without a full-field theorem.
