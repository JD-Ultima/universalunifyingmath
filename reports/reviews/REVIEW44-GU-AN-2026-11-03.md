**Verdict: AN1–AN3 are accepted under their stated hypotheses. No correction is required.** This is an obstruction for the specified reconstructed bosonic equation on entire constant-odd, single-kernel domains of positive Z1. It is not a general Lambda5 or physical-theory verdict.

The tensor differentiation in AN1 retains the full covariant jet. Directly,
$$\iota_b\operatorname{Alt}J=J_b-\sum_a e^a\wedge\iota_bJ_a,$$
so exterior/contraction adjunction gives
$$\operatorname{div}S=\langle\operatorname{Ctr}J,\iota_b\omega\rangle
+\tfrac12dN-\langle\iota_b\operatorname{Alt}J,\omega\rangle.$$
There is no missing form-degree factor or suppressed Cartan component.

For the exterior copy, AL gives $\operatorname{Alt}J=0$, $\operatorname{Ctr}J=-17F_4/9$ and $dN=157\xi/18$, with $\langle F_4,\iota_b\omega\rangle=\xi_b$. Hence $\operatorname{div}S=89\xi/36$ and
$$q\,dN+\ell\operatorname{div}S=\tfrac{1768}{3}\xi.$$
For the contraction-Gamma copy, the **right** Gamma convention gives $\operatorname{Alt}J=-22F_8\Gamma/15$, $\operatorname{Ctr}J=0$ and $dN=8\eta/5$. The pairing $\langle\iota_b(F_8\Gamma),\omega\rangle=-\eta_b$ supplies the essential minus sign. Thus $\operatorname{div}S=-2\eta/3$ and the Bianchi residual is $256\eta/3$. Both coefficients are nonzero. Since $b,q,\ell$ are constant and $G$ is the actual Einstein tensor, contracted Bianchi forces both relevant currents, and hence $dN$, to vanish. No extra field equation or base-metric variation is assumed.

Here $N_0$ is a fixed real coefficient; neither the field nor its norm function is extended.
