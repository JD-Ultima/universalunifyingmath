## Limits of acceptance

under flat positive Z1, lambda=1/5, the declared compact WA pure odd branch
and constant nonzero kappa. Higher quadratic grades, covariant closure,
full pointwise bosonic equations and local/global fields are not established.
The result neither classifies arbitrary mixed values nor solves the general
pure-quintic Gram problem. It makes no quotient, curved-base, other-parameter,
other-sector, physical or literature-priority claim. Classical invariant
theory remains an explicit imported input. No old main, suite, higher-grade
investigation or git operation was run for this review; external peer review
remains outstanding.
