## Verdict

The exclusions and existence statements of S16 survive the repairs below. The original proof of S14 had two substantive continuation gaps: a constant-coefficient exceptional amplitude, and an unjustified assumption that a path avoiding a branch locus could reach the radial curve. The repaired proof uses a finite algebraic cover and a local pole-order argument. S8's continuation is valid after making its closedness and initial analyticity explicit.

The statement that a plane-wave base is Einstein **exactly** on its parallel set was stronger than the argument supplied. It is removed: the parallel set is Einstein, evaluated points outside it fail, and S5 excludes Einstein open sets. The entire pointwise locus is not classified here.

## Findings and applied repairs

**R15-S1 — constant kappa was omitted from the finite-fibre argument.** S14 step 3 asserted that tau is an algebraic branch with a finite inverse image under kappa. For the permitted twisted amplitude epsilon=1, A=11/6, kappa is the constant -3/289. At that value, `18*k*D1^2+N2` is identically zero when k=-3/289. Consequently neither the stated quadratic nor its finite-fibre conclusion applies.

The repair treats this amplitude first. The rational identity xi=kappa*v-flat continues to the radial points without introducing tau. Q6 then forces sigma_g=0 and B=-289/3, or lambda=337/240, outside the (14,0) range. In (7,7) the corresponding A=-11/6 is impossible for real nonzero a. The exception is excluded, and the remainder has a nonconstant rational map kappa:P1->P1.

**R15-S2 — ramification and poles needed a proof.** The original path argument removed a proper algebraic subset but did not show that the radial curve lay outside it. A radial curve can lie entirely in a branch locus. The assertion that the primitive has logarithmic singularities also omitted degree drops: at epsilon*A=119/144, Q2 is linear and the differential has a double pole at infinity. At epsilon*A=1 it is regular at infinity.

The continuation lemma now constructs the normalization of the component of the finite base change `M x_(k,P1,kappa) P1` containing the initial branch. It continues the identity

`tau*(eta)=pi*(omega)`, with `eta=576 D1/Q2 d tau` and `omega=v'-flat|F`.

The form omega is holomorphic at the radial full-rank points. A pole of order m under ramification of order e pulls back to a pole of order e(m-1)+1, which is positive. Thus a pole of eta cannot appear over such a point. This uses codimension-one local parameters on the normal cover and also applies at infinity using 1/tau.

On a curve over a radial arc with constant k, tau is constant because a nonconstant rational map P1->P1 has finite fibres. Its value is not a pole of eta. The restriction argument is made with holomorphic functions to cover singular points of the normalization: finite separable dominant pullback is injective on rational differential forms, so the differential identity first gives d(omega)=0. Locally write omega=df and eta=dH. The holomorphic function H(tau)-f(pi) is locally constant on the dense regular locus and extends as that constant on the normal component. Restricting this function equality to the normalized curve shows that f is constant on the radial curve, hence omega vanishes there. This contradicts its displayed nonzero Euler component and avoids restricting Kähler differentials through singularities. For nonconstant radial k, restriction to the normalized curve gives Q7(D) on a dense open arc; Q8's polynomial certificate then applies. No assumption of an unramified radial curve or a globally single-valued logarithm remains. The same repair is used in S15.

**R15-S3 — S8's smooth-to-holomorphic step was implicit.** The vertical rational form alpha=mu1*v'-flat is closed on the initial real ball because it equals d(log r); its exterior derivative therefore vanishes as a rational identity on the complex fibre. Local holomorphic primitives show that the initial smooth nonzero r is fibre-analytic. Its nonzero germs continue by the exponential integral along paths. Monodromy is allowed; the proof needs local germs at the endpoint, not a global primitive. The Laurent-polynomial contradiction on the radial arc is then valid.

**R15-S4 — pointwise scope and rational poles.** The plane-wave equality-of-loci assertion is replaced by the proven inclusion, finite counterchecks and no-open-set conclusion. S4(b)'s rational identities are qualified to points where their rational expressions are regular, in the real fibre as well as the complex fibre.

## Mathematical review and remaining scope

S1's Euler identities and S4's polynomiality/dichotomy follow from the displayed metric and minors argument. S2's parallel-set transfer is sound locally. S6's Schur reduction separates its linear and quadratic terms: W1 and 2W1 separate degrees, nonzero projections generate the inequivalent summands, and two independent invariant pairs resolve the repeated trivial summand. The finite checks are not being used as a proof without that representation-theoretic reduction. The null-orbit reduction in S5 is consistent with the Lorentzian type-N orbit and the eight neutral sign classes. Those large fibre-jet calculations were reproduced through S's own code, not independently rebuilt in this review.

The unresolved class remains the twisted cone at lambda=1/5 in (14,0), away from Pi, with rank Ric_g<=1. The useful next question is whether the relaxed system has a full-rank inconsistent fibre point for **every** nonflat jet in that class, or whether exceptional jets survive. A general answer must address the Weyl tensor and the remaining connection jets; the specified rank-one example cannot substitute for those quantifiers. The slaving, det M!=0, invariant-odd-sector and n=4 hypotheses remain in force.
