[3] The 1/n^2 ladder: reconstruction and the choice that fixes the exponent
  Source: 'the held pendant pair at rank n is the washed symmetric power of n less one passages
  along the bond ... the register of the ladder equal to the dimension of the register of the pair';
  'the two-vertex ladder equals one over the rank squared at all eight ranks'.
  Reconstruction R: the washed (twirled) state on Sym^(n-1)(C^m) is I/dim; a two-vertex term reads it
  twice, giving (1/dim)^2. With m = 2 (the common support of the (2,3) pair) dim = n.
  m=2: dim Sym^(n-1)(C^2) = n; ladder (1/dim)^2 = n**(-2); equals 1/n^2 for n=1..8: True
  m=3: dim Sym^(n-1)(C^3) = n*(n + 1)/2; ladder (1/dim)^2 = 4/(n**2*(n + 1)**2); equals 1/n^2 for n=1..8: False
  m=4: dim Sym^(n-1)(C^4) = n*(n + 1)*(n + 2)/6; ladder (1/dim)^2 = 36/(n**2*(n + 1)**2*(n + 2)**2); equals 1/n^2 for n=1..8: False
