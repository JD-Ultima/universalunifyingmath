# Open Specification Gaps — Chain Theory, α Letter

Each gap is a point where the number depends on a choice the axiom does not fix. Where we computed alternatives, the results are in `formalization/chain-theory/alpha/audit.md`.

| ID | Gap | Blocks | Candidate resolutions / what would close it |
|---|---|---|---|
| CT-G01 | **Status of the weight law.** $k_r=3^{[X=-1]}2^{[Y=-1]}$ is introduced by an argument ("amplitude $1/\sqrt d$ summed over $d$ matched directions gives $\sqrt d$"). It is not derived as the trace of an operator built independently of the records: $M$ is *defined* to have eigenvalue $\sqrt{k_r}$ on record $r$ and $0$ elsewhere. | CT-016, CT-017 | Construct $M$ as an explicit operator on $R$ (from the projections and the twirl) and show that its diagonal on the record basis is $\sqrt{k_r}$. |
| CT-G02 | **Which end an exchange "acts at".** On every product record, $X$ and $Y$ are the *same* involution $E$ (F-CT-2). They differ only in which records they are said to act on and which dimension, 3 or 2, they contribute. Three wording clauses fix this assignment (S Table S-inputs). Rewording them gives 1/value from 112.4 to 146.5. | CT-016 | An operator-level definition of "the end at which an exchange acts" that does not refer to the wording. |
| CT-G03 | **Conjugation convention at the far end.** The pair is invariant under $U\otimes\bar U$, yet the identification algebra acts as $\xi_1\otimes 1+1\otimes\xi_2$ and is made traceless on $\mathbb C^{d_1}\oplus\mathbb C^{d_2}$. Replacing $\xi_2\to\bar\xi_2$ on the far block does not preserve the trace condition. With it, $(2,2)$ also has an acting central charge, and the link's hypercharge becomes $5/6$ instead of $1/6$ (F-CT-3). | CT-007, CT-009, CT-012 | Derive from the axiom which block carries $\bar\xi$, or show that the selection is invariant. |
| CT-G04 | **Origin of the direct sum.** The links live on $\mathbb C^{d_1}\otimes\mathbb C^{d_2}$. The trace condition defining the $u(1)$ is imposed on $\mathbb C^{d_1}\oplus\mathbb C^{d_2}$, a space the axiom does not contain. S4 justifies it by "sees no global phase of a pair". Reading that phase on the matched directions instead ($x+y=0$) gives *no* acting charge for any type, so the object would not exist (F-CT-3, reading B). | CT-007, CT-012 | Give an axiom-level reason for the direct-sum trace. |
| CT-G05 | **Normalization $N$.** $\dim R$ counts the whole 36-dim operator space at read links and the 24-dim traceless part at the unread link. Alternatives (all-whole $36^3$, all-traceless $24^3$, traceless-plus-identity…) give 1/value of 205.6, 60.9, 129.5, 188.9 (F-CT-4). These are not listed in S Table S-alternatives. | CT-013, CT-017 | Add them to the alternatives table with the exclusion argument. |
| CT-G06 | **Hypercharge vs electric charge.** The acting charge is $\mathcal Q=6iY$, the hypercharge direction. $\alpha(0)$ is the coupling of $U(1)_\text{em}$, with $e=g'\cos\theta_W$. No map from the hypercharge readout to $e^2/4\pi$ is given; the Letter defers it ("lies beyond this Letter"). In the Standard Model the ratio $\alpha_\text{em}/\alpha_Y=\cos^2\theta_W\approx0.77$ is far from 1. | CT-020 | Derive electroweak mixing in-model, or explain why the hypercharge-link readout equals $\alpha_\text{em}(0)$ rather than $\alpha_Y$ at some scale. |
| CT-G07 | **On-shell identification.** QED's $Z_3$ is regulator- and scheme-dependent, and $\alpha_\text{bare}$ is not observable. The model sets $\alpha_\text{bare}=1$ by its unit. The correspondence to the on-shell scheme is declared, not derived (S8: "is a definition … used in no proof"). | CT-019, CT-020 | A model-internal propagator and momentum dependence, with a pole at zero transfer. |
| CT-G08 | **Amplitude vs Born weight.** Weights enter as $\sqrt{k_r}$ ("two letters = a two-point quantity"), not $k_r$ (1/value 97.5). This is an argued interpretive step, not a theorem. | CT-016 | As CT-G01. |
| CT-G09 | **Commutator records.** They are the single-slot operators $\sigma_\lambda^{(q_1)}-\sigma_\lambda^{(q_2)}$ (up to $2i$). The Letter's own rule excludes single-letter insertions, and these are admitted as an exception. Their count of 3 is basis-independent at the qubit. Only the difference is kept, not the sum. Variants give 1/value 132.7–141.6. | CT-014, CT-017 | A uniform definition of "record" that yields exactly these 172 without an exception clause. |
| CT-G10 | **History of the construction.** The paper does not record whether the rules were fixed before the value was computed. This determines the effective trial factor (F-CT-4). | CT-020 evidential weight | Pre-registration of future rule changes and predictions; a version history of rule choices. |
| CT-G11 | **Basis covariance of the records.** The Letter's exchange identifies the two read links' bases by the identity map. That identification does not commute with the symmetry group $G_\Omega$ of the joint state, so the Letter's $E$-even/odd splitting and its commutator-record span are not $G_\Omega$-invariant (F-CT-10). | CT-014, CT-015 | **Resolved without change of value.** The covariant exchange $E'=E\circ T$ gives an invariant 172-dimensional record space with the same sector sizes. The Letter should define the exchange with the transpose. |
| CT-G12 | **Freedom left by symmetry.** Invariant dressings of the covariant record space form a 22-dimensional commutant with 10 complex (9 real, for Hermitian-preserving dressings) independent trace parameters (F-CT-10). The weight law picks one point. It distinguishes two copies of $(1,3)_-$ (weights 1 and $\sqrt6$), and symmetry does not supply that distinction. | CT-016 | A principle beyond the wording clauses that selects this point. |

*2026-10-30 note on CT-G01 (and CT-G12), from `formalization/chain-theory/alpha/operator-origin.md` (`computation/chain-theory/alpha/operator_origin.py`):* CT-G01's candidate resolution is met without closing the gap. On $R$ the Letter's $M$ equals $(1+(\sqrt3-1)S\Pi_X)(1+(\sqrt2-1)S\Pi_Y)$, where $S=\tfrac12(1+(-1)^{\hat N}E')$, $\Pi_X=[\hat N_Q\hat N_q=0]$, $\Pi_Y=[\hat N_q=1]$ and the occupation numbers $\hat N$ are built from partial traces. But $S\Pi_X$ gives the qutrit amplitude to 9 of the 12 records with qubit letters only (the 6 E′-even qubit–qubit and the 3 commutator records), which is the end-rule clause of CT-G02. No word of length ≤ 3 in 38 primitives (56 355 words) reproduces the weights as an operator, as its diagonal or as its per-isotypic traces, and no survival × attribution dressing local in the original frame (of 321 489) does. A Galois lemma extends the $\sqrt{k_r}$, trace and value negatives to all lengths and all $\mathbb Q(i)$-linear combinations; a qutrit|qubit factorization theorem extends the $k_r$ negative to all products of factorizing letters and their twirls; for words with $\Pi_\pm$ or a read link the $k_r$, $|C|$ and $\sqrt C$ negatives hold up to length 3. Locality is frame-relative: see the next note. Sharpened closure criterion: an operator construction in which the end attribution is not an input. CT-G12: the two $(1,3)_-$ copies are separated by $\hat N$, an operator; their weights are not fixed by it. Status: CT-G01 reduces to CT-G02 (and CT-G08); open.

*2026-10-30 note on CT-G01 and CT-G02, from `formalization/chain-theory/alpha/transport.md` (`computation/chain-theory/alpha/transport.py`):* The direction left untested in the previous note is now tested: carry the qubit letters to the qutrit copies through the read links, then credit the join. The transport is unique, $t(B)=\iota B^{\mathsf T}\iota^\dagger$ ($\sigma_{x,y,z}\mapsto\lambda_1,-\lambda_2,\lambda_3$, supported on the 2-dim common support $S$). It is equivariant only under $H=SU(2)\times U(1)\subset G_\Omega$; its $G_\Omega$-equivariant part is 0. It carries the 9 qubit–qubit records onto QQ records of the same E′-parity, so in the transported frame the qutrit credit of these records is local. A record-wise construction then reproduces $M$ exactly (6 of 338 688 frame-local choices), but only with two inputs. First, the exchange that credits the qubit–qubit records is assessed in the transported frame and the one that credits the mixed records in the original one (for the Letter's domains, X and Y); a single frame fails (Theorem 6). Second, the join is credited with $\sqrt3$, although a transported letter reaches two of its three directions. Under $H$ the credit is $2/\sqrt3$ or $\sqrt2$, which gives 1/value 140.702 or 139.030 (Theorem 5). Transporting every record is many-to-one, so no compression $T^\dagger DT$ and no Rayleigh quotient of any dressing $D$ gives the weights (Theorem 4). Selective transport needs the selector of the 12 qubit-only records. No word of length ≤ 3 with a transport letter (5953 distinct compressions) reproduces the weights. Status: CT-G01 open. CT-G02 now has an operator-level reading (the frame), with the frame assignment and the join credit as its two remaining choices.

*2026-10-30 note on CT-G10, from `formalization/chain-theory/alpha/mechanical-trial-factor.md` (`computation/chain-theory/alpha/mechanical_mutations.py`, Lean check `proofs/lean/chain-theory/mutation_spotcheck.sh`):* F-CT-4's rule space B was declared by hand. It is now replaced by a space generated mechanically from `ChainAlpha.lean`. Fifteen declared mutation operators (literals, tests, connectives, quantifiers, branches, match arms, arithmetic operators, end/role labels, list entries) are applied at all 425 sites of the 44 definitions the readout depends on. This gives 818 single mutants and 331 490 pairs with disjoint sites. Every mutant is evaluated exactly by a Python transcription, which reproduces `upstream_mutations_lean.out` line for line and agrees with Lean 4.35.0 on all 818 singles and a seeded sample of 300 pairs. The 272 988 well-defined mutants take 10 340 distinct values. Their density in $1/\text{value}\in(120,155)$ is 31.6 per unit, against 103.9 for B, so the expected number of chance values within $\pm1\sigma$ of CODATA 2022 is $1.3\times10^{-6}$ (B: $4.4\times10^{-6}$; both spaces together: $5.6\times10^{-6}$). No mutant other than the Letter's value comes within relative $10^{-4}$; the nearest, $1/\text{value}=137.0569$, is $9.9\times10^5\sigma$ away. The trial-factor conclusion of F-CT-4 is therefore robust to the choice between a hand-declared and a mechanically generated space. Neither space contains multi-token rewordings beyond pairs, the amplitude/Born and $N$ choices that are not definitions in the file, the selection conventions, or anything tried before the file was written. Status: CT-G10 open. The closure criterion is unchanged: a record of the order in which rules and value were fixed.
