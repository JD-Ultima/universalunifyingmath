# Observations and research questions — Howard arithmetic and geometry

Entries distinguish reproducible mathematical observations from questions about source interpretation. A reconstruction is attributed to the source only when primary material supports that attribution. Claim IDs are retained for traceability.

| ID | Documented observation | Limits and next question |
|---|---|---|
| P-TH-1 | In the units quoted in TH-S2, 40.5, 81, 162, 324, and 186,624 have only 2 and 3 as prime factors when expressed as rationals. The other two recorded dimensional figures do not. See the frequency list and factorizations in the numerical computation. | The four frequencies form one doubling sequence. These figures are neither independent samples nor invariant under a change of units. They establish an arithmetic pattern in the quoted numbers; a physical explanation would require a defined observable and mechanism. |
| P-TH-2 | With ordinary addition and the hypotheses of Theorem A4, the operation a ⊗ b = 2ab on integers is isomorphic to ordinary multiplication on even integers by x ↦ 2x. | Could the primary text's definition support this pair-counting reconstruction? The isomorphism is proved; its attribution as the intended meaning is unresolved. |
| P-TH-3 | The log₂-semiring reconstruction satisfies the specified H1 and H2 identities with its own addition. See arithmetic.md §5 and semirings.out. | Does a primary definition identify the operation with composition of frequency intervals? Frequency-related quotations alone do not establish that interpretation. |
| P-TH-4 | The tested sphere/window interpretation matches the tetrahedron and cube counts, but the octahedron gives (6,8), against the recorded “30 poles.” See lynchpin_geometry.out. | This interpretation fails at n = 6. A different interpretation needs an explicit counting rule and a test on additional cases. |
| P-TH-5 | The geometric reconstruction compares regular-pentagon angles with the angles of the Plateau T-cone; those angles differ. See geometry.md T1–T2. | Do primary drawings, CAD data, or measured models satisfy Plateau's angle conditions? The present material does not establish that the physical models do. |
| P-TH-6 | For c > 0, √c is a fixed point of x ↦ x³/c. The stated √2 iteration is an instance of that identity. See numerics.md N1. | This is an arithmetic result. A claimed application would require additional definitions and evidence. |
| P-TH-7 | The Chain Theory denominator 31,104 = 2⁷3⁵ has the same allowed prime factors as the figures in P-TH-1. Its factors arise from the construction's qudit dimensions. | A shared factorization does not establish a structural relationship between the frameworks. Any proposed relationship needs an explicit map and a test. |
