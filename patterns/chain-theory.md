# Pattern catalogue — Chain Theory

These are conjectures and observations only. None is a result until it is formalized in `formalization/`.

| ID | Pattern | Status |
|---|---|---|
| CT-P01 | The read cap $d_1d_2\le6$ (decomposability of all positive maps, equivalently PPT ⇔ separable) singles out $(2,2)$ and $(2,3)$ among end dimensions ≥2. Applying the Letter's direct-sum trace convention to the latter gives $S(U(2)\times U(3))\cong(SU(2)\times SU(3)\times U(1))/\mathbb Z_6$. This identifies an abstract group with the usual Standard Model global form, conditional on that convention; it does not derive a physical gauge theory. | Group identification from imported theorems plus the stated trace convention. Physical interpretation open (CT-G03/G04/G06). |
